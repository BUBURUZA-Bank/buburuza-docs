---
title: "Recipients and Beneficiaries API"
description: "Managing reusable payee records and the provider links behind them."
---

All paths below sit under `/api/v1/app`.

These are two layers of one idea. A **recipient** is your record of who is paid. A **beneficiary** is the link between that record and the provider's own account reference. Keeping them separate means a provider changing a reference does not force you to rebuild the payee.

## Recipients

```text
GET    /recipients                   → list
POST   /recipients                   → create
PATCH  /recipients/{recipient_id}    → update
```

Create a recipient once and reuse it. A withdrawal names a recipient rather than carrying bank details inline, so payee data lives in one place and is corrected in one place.

## Beneficiaries

```text
GET  /beneficiaries                                          → list
GET  /beneficiaries/{beneficiary_id}                         → one
POST /beneficiaries                                          → create
POST /beneficiaries/{beneficiary_id}/unlink-recipient        → detach from a recipient
POST /beneficiaries/{beneficiary_id}/relink-recipient        → attach to a recipient
```

Unlink and relink exist for one situation: the provider's reference changed, but the payee did not. Repair the link rather than deleting the recipient — deleting loses the history that ties past payouts to that payee.
