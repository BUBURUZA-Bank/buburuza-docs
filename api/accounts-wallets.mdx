---
title: "Accounts and Wallets API"
description: "Reading accounts, wallets, balances, funding methods, and deposit instructions."
---

All paths below sit under `/api/v1/app`.

## Accounts

```text
GET /accounts                → accounts in the active space
GET /accounts/{account_id}   → one account
```

Check `status` and `money_movement_enabled` before attempting any payment. An account that is not active will refuse money movement with `403`, and no retry will change that until verification and provisioning complete.

## Wallets

```text
GET /accounts/{account_id}/wallets          → wallets on the account
GET /wallets/{wallet_id}                    → one wallet
GET /wallets/{wallet_id}/balance-history    → balance over time
```

Each wallet is `fiat` or `stablecoin`. A fiat wallet receives over payment rails; a stablecoin wallet receives at a deposit address.

## Balances

A balance has three components, all decimal strings:

| Component | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `available` | Spendable now — the figure payments are authorised against |
| `pending` | In flight, not yet settled |
| `hold` | Reserved by operations already under way |

Price and authorise against `available`. A value visible in `pending` is not spendable.

## Funding

```text
GET /accounts/{account_id}/funding-methods       → open methods for this account
GET /wallets/{wallet_id}/deposit-instructions    → how to pay into this wallet
```

Fiat instructions carry the account details for the chosen rail. Crypto instructions carry a deposit address with its chain and asset — send only that asset on that chain.

## Payment account status

```text
GET  /payment-account/status   → provisioning state
POST /payment-account/start    → begin provisioning where it is not automatic
GET  /payment-agreement/link   → hosted provider agreement, when one is required
```
