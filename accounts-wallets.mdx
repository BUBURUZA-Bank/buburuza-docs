# Accounts & Wallets

## Organisation payment account

Every verified organisation gets one **payment account**, provisioned automatically after KYB passes. The account is the umbrella for everything money-related: wallets, balances, statements, and bank letters.

| Property | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `status` | `active` once provisioning completes; money movement is enabled only when active |
| `money_movement_enabled` | Whether transfers, payouts, and funding are allowed |
| `legal_entity` | The organisation the account belongs to |

## Wallets

Each account holds two kinds of wallets:

| Kind | Rails | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fiat` | ACH, wire | Hold fiat balances, receive deposits, fund payouts |
| `stablecoin` | Crypto deposit address | Hold stablecoin balances, receive crypto top-ups |

A default fiat and a default stablecoin wallet are created automatically when the account activates. You can provision additional wallets per account.

## Balances

Balances are returned as **decimal strings** to avoid floating-point errors, with three components:

- `available` — what you can spend right now,
- `pending` — funds in flight (deposits not yet settled, holds),
- `hold` — amounts reserved by in-flight operations.

## Statements & documents

- **Bank letter** — a document confirming your account details, available per account.
- **Statements** — per-period account statements.

Both are available in the dashboard and through the API.

## Limits & tiers

Your KYB tier determines what your account can do (light vs full verification). Until KYB passes and the account is active, money movement endpoints return `403`.
