# Money Movement

## Funding (money in)

Top-ups are a **two-step** flow: pick a method, then fetch instructions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a funding method">
    Select **fiat** (ACH or wire) or **crypto** for the wallet you want to fund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get instructions">
    Fiat: account and routing details for the selected rail. Crypto: a deposit address with chain and asset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send funds">
    Initiate the transfer from your bank or wallet. Inbound money appears in your transaction list automatically once it clears — there is no deposit step on our side.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transfers

A transfer moves money between wallets.

1. **Fee preview** — price the transfer before it exists. The returned quote is bound to the amount and route.
2. **Create** — submit the transfer with the `fee_quote_id` from the preview.
3. **Track** — poll the transfer status.

<Note>
The fee is charged **on top** of the amount you enter: the recipient receives exactly what you typed, and you are debited amount + fee. Quotes expire after ten minutes and are consumed by a single payment.
</Note>

## Payouts (withdrawals)

Withdrawals send money to an external recipient:

1. Create a **recipient** once (or reuse an existing one).
2. Get a **fee preview** for the withdrawal.
3. Create the withdrawal and track it.

## Recipients & beneficiaries

- **Recipients** are reusable payee records you manage (create, update, delete).
- **Beneficiaries** link your recipients to provider-side accounts, including unlinking and relinking when a provider changes a reference.

## Transactions

Every money event — deposit, transfer, payout, fee — lands in the **transactions** list with a `kind` and `status`. Filter by account, wallet, kind, or status; each transaction carries its recipient and timestamps.

## Rates

Current exchange rates are available as a read-only endpoint, so your product can show up-to-date conversion numbers without calling elsewhere.
