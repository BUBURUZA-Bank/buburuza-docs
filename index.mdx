## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [buburuza.com](https://buburuza.com), verify your email, and complete identity verification. 85% of applications are approved in under 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet">
    Deposit fiat via bank transfer or card, or bridge crypto assets from Ethereum or Arbitrum directly into your Buburuza wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start transacting">
    Send money globally, invest in tokenized assets, or use the API to embed financial services in your own application.
  </Step>
</Steps>
