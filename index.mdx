## Before you start

- **Check availability.** Onboarding serves companies registered in the **United States or Canada**, and individuals resident in those two countries. There is no waiting list for other countries yet.
- **Complete verification.** Until your verification passes and your payment account activates, you can sign in and complete onboarding, but you cannot hold or move funds.

Read the [platform introduction](/introduction) and [onboarding and security guide](/onboarding-and-security) for details.

<Note>
  Buburuza is a financial technology company, **not a bank**. Regulated money movement is performed by licensed partners, and balances are not covered by deposit insurance. See [Legal and regulatory](/legal).
</Note>
