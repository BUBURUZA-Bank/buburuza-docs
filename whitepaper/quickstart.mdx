This guide takes you from zero to your first transaction as quickly as possible. Buburuza handles most of the setup automatically, so you can focus on getting started rather than configuring settings. Follow these four steps and you'll be up and running in minutes.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [buburuza.com](https://buburuza.com) and create your account with your email address. You'll receive a confirmation link — click it to verify your email.

    You can also add a phone number at this stage for account recovery purposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete identity verification">
    Buburuza uses Sumsub to verify your identity. You'll need:

    - A valid government-issued ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID)
    - A few seconds for a liveness check — a short selfie video

    **85% of applicants are approved within 30 seconds.** Complex cases are escalated to a human reviewer. Once approved, you get immediate access to your account.

    <Note>
      Make sure your ID photo is clear and unobstructed. Blurry or cropped images are the most common cause of delays.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your account">
    Add money to your account using any of these methods:

    - **Bank transfer** — SEPA or SWIFT from any bank account
    - **Debit or credit card** — instant deposit with standard card processing fees
    - **Crypto bridge** — move assets from Ethereum or Arbitrum directly into your Buburuza wallet

    Your funds are held in multi-currency accounts supporting USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD, as well as Buburuza's native stablecoins USDB and EURB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first transaction">
    Once your account is funded, you're ready to transact:

    - **Send money** — transfer fiat or stablecoins to any email address or wallet
    - **Pay globally** — cross-border transfers settle in under a second via Buburuza-Chain for less than \$0.001
    - **Use your virtual card** — a virtual debit card is issued instantly for online purchases

    That's it. Your account is active and fully functional.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do right away

Your access level depends on which verification tier you've completed. Here's what's available from day one versus what requires enhanced KYC.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="After basic verification">
    Available immediately after your initial ID check:

    - Send and receive fiat and stablecoins (standard limits apply)
    - Hold multi-currency balances (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, USDB, EURB)
    - Use a virtual debit card for online purchases
    - Access AI-powered spending insights and alerts
    - Set up transaction notifications and security preferences
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="After enhanced verification">
    Unlocked when you complete additional documentation:

    - Higher transaction limits and daily transfer caps
    - Physical debit card issuance
    - Access to investment products (stocks, ETFs)
    - Crypto-collateralized lending
    - \$BUB token utility and fee discounts
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="After premium verification">
    Full platform access with complete documentation on file:

    - Real-World Asset (RWA) investments via Buburuza-Labs
    - Investment in tokenized assets (minimum \$1,000)
    - Premium concierge banking services
    - Institutional-grade custody for large crypto holdings
    - Advanced API access for developer integrations
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  You can complete enhanced verification at any time from **Settings → Verification**. The process typically takes under five minutes if you have your documents ready.
</Tip>

## Key features at a glance

### AI banking

Buburuza's B1 Engine runs a network of specialized AI agents that handle account management, fraud detection, and financial recommendations automatically. In practice, this means:

- **Natural language payments** — describe a transfer in plain text and the AI handles the details
- **Spending insights** — automatic categorization and cash flow forecasting
- **Proactive fraud detection** — behavioral monitoring that flags anomalies in real time
- **24/7 AI support** — get answers and take actions through the in-app assistant at any time

### Stablecoins

USDB and EURB are Buburuza's native stablecoins, each backed 1:1 by Circle's USDC and EURC respectively. They're designed for everyday payments and cross-border transfers:

- Transfers settle in under one second on Buburuza-Chain
- Transaction fees are less than \$0.001
- Balances earn yield automatically when held in your account
- Accepted anywhere that supports standard stablecoins

### \$BUB token

$$BUB is the native utility and governance token for Buburuza-Chain. As a Buburuza customer, you can use $$BUB to:

- Pay for network fees at a discount compared to standard rates
- Participate in governance votes on platform changes
- Access exclusive features and higher-tier benefits

$$BUB is an ERC-20 token on Arbitrum, bridged to Buburuza-Chain (Layer 3). You can acquire $$BUB through in-app purchase or by bridging from Ethereum.
