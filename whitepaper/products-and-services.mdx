## Core banking platform

### Multi-currency accounts

Your Buburuza account holds fiat, stablecoins, and cryptocurrency in one place.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Fiat currencies" icon="money-bill">
    Hold and transact in USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD with competitive exchange rates and transparent fee structures. Each currency gets its own IBAN for local and international banking operations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stablecoins — USDB and EURB" icon="coins">
    Buburuza's native stablecoins are backed 1:1 by Circle's USDC and EURC. You can mint USDB or EURB by depositing the equivalent stablecoin, and redeem them at any time. They settle on Buburuza-Chain in under a second at a network cost of less than \$0.001 per transaction; the Buburuza service fee is quoted separately before you confirm.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cryptocurrency" icon="bitcoin">
    Hold Bitcoin, Ethereum, and major altcoins with institutional-grade custody via Fireblocks. You can choose between non-custodial wallets (you hold your keys) or Fireblocks-managed custodial wallets for additional protection.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Payment solutions

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Instant transfers">
    Transfers between Buburuza accounts settle on Buburuza-Chain in under a second. Network cost is under \$0.001 per transaction; the Buburuza service fee is shown before you confirm. No cutoff times, no business-day delays, no correspondent banking.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Global remittances">
    Send money internationally via stablecoins—bypassing the traditional correspondent banking network. Processing time is under one second on-chain, with fiat delivery in minutes. The full fee is quoted before you confirm.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Traditional rails">
    When you need to send to a traditional bank account, Buburuza routes through SWIFT for international wires (1–3 business days) or SEPA for European transfers (1 business day). AI-optimized routing selects the fastest and cheapest available path.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Smart contracts">
    Set up programmable payments with conditional execution and automated scheduling. Use smart contracts for recurring payroll, escrow arrangements, or DeFi strategies—without writing code yourself.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## AI-powered financial services

### Intelligent account management

Your Buburuza account doesn't just store money—it actively helps you manage it.

**Predictive analytics:**

- **Cash flow forecasting** — the AI analyzes your transaction patterns to predict upcoming account balances and alert you before a potential overdraft
- **Bill recognition** — recurring payments are automatically detected and surfaced with optimization recommendations
- **Savings optimization** — idle funds are identified and you're shown opportunities to move them into yield-bearing accounts
- **Account type recommendations** — as your usage evolves, the AI suggests account structures that better match your needs

**Automated financial health:**

- **Expense categorization** — every transaction is classified automatically by a machine learning model that improves with your feedback
- **Budget optimization** — dynamic recommendations based on your income patterns and stated financial goals
- **Debt management** — AI-driven strategies for consolidation and payment order optimization
- **Goal tracking** — set a savings or investment target and receive automated progress updates with milestone notifications

### Advanced security and fraud prevention

Buburuza's Security AI runs continuously in the background without interrupting your experience.

**Behavioral biometrics:**

| Signal | How it's used |
| --- | --- |
| Typing patterns | Keystroke dynamics analyzed for continuous authentication |
| Device interaction | Mouse movement and touch pattern recognition |
| Session analytics | Navigation behavior and timing analysis |
| Risk scoring | Real-time fraud probability with adaptive thresholds |

**Proactive security measures:**

- **Account takeover prevention** — multi-factor verification triggers automatically when risk indicators fire, before any unauthorized action can complete
- **Transaction anomaly detection** — real-time flagging of unusual transaction amounts, recipients, or timing
- **Device fingerprinting** — your trusted devices are registered so unfamiliar devices are immediately flagged
- **Geographic risk assessment** — location-based security controls with travel notifications you control

<Tip>
  You can register new trusted devices proactively in your account settings to avoid friction when traveling or switching phones.
</Tip>

### Real-world asset investment — Buburuza-Labs

Buburuza-Labs tokenizes real-world industrial assets so you can invest in productive enterprises that generate actual cash flows—not speculation.

**What you can invest in:**

  </Card>

  <Card title="Agricultural assets" icon="wheat">
    Farmland, processing facilities, and agricultural equipment with seasonal income distributions.
  </Card>

**How the investment process works:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse available assets">
    Each listed asset includes an independent valuation, a due diligence report, expected monthly revenue, and token price. Minimum investment is \$1,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase tokens">
    Buy ERC-20 tokens representing your proportional ownership share. Tokens are issued on Buburuza-Chain and held in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive monthly revenue">
    Net revenue from the asset—after operating costs—is distributed monthly to all token holders in USDB, automatically via smart contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor your investment">
    Real-time GPS tracking, automated usage logging, monthly financial reports, and quarterly independent audits are all published on-chain and surfaced in your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Revenue distributions are paid in USDB (Buburuza's USD-pegged stablecoin) and appear in your account automatically each month. You can redeem USDB for USD at any time.
</Note>

### Lending and credit services

Buburuza offers AI-optimized credit products that blend traditional lending with blockchain-based collateralization.

**Crypto-collateralized loans:**

Use your existing crypto holdings to unlock liquidity without selling:

- Post \$BUB, BTC, ETH, or RWA tokens as collateral
- Receive a fiat or stablecoin loan against that collateral
- Collateral health is monitored continuously by the Risk AI—you'll be notified before any margin call
- All loan terms are tokenized and auditable on-chain

**Traditional lending:**

- **Personal loans** — AI-scored approvals with flexible repayment schedules and competitive rates based on your full financial history
- **Business credit** — SME lending with streamlined applications and rapid decisions, up to the limits of your verified business profile
- **Credit lines** — revolving credit facilities backed by crypto or traditional assets, available when you need them

**What makes Buburuza's lending different:**

- Approvals are instant and AI-driven—no waiting for a loan officer
- All loan agreements are tokenized, making repayment terms fully auditable
- On-chain credit history builds over time, improving your terms for future borrowing
- Continuous AI monitoring of your repayment ability means proactive alerts, not surprise defaults
