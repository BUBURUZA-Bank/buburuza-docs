Buburuza doesn't run on customer service scripts or rigid rule engines — it runs on B1, a multi-agent artificial intelligence system purpose-built for banking. Six specialized AI agents operate simultaneously on your account, each an expert in its own domain. They predict cash shortfalls before they happen, route your payments along the fastest and cheapest path, detect fraud in milliseconds, and answer your questions at 2 a.m. in your own language. This page explains what each agent does for you and how the system is designed to keep you in control while doing the heavy lifting.

## What B1 does for you

B1 is organized into six service layers. Each layer is a distinct AI agent trained for a specific job. They share information through secure internal channels, so a fraud signal caught by the Security agent can immediately inform the Payments agent — without any human needing to relay the message.

## The six agents, explained

### Account Management AI

The Account Management agent monitors your income, recurring expenses, and spending habits to give you a financial picture that's always current — and always forward-looking.

**What it does for you:**

- **Cash flow forecasting.** Using pattern recognition across your transaction history, the agent predicts your account balance days ahead. If a large bill and a low-income week are about to collide, you get an alert before the shortfall — not after.
- **Savings optimization.** When idle funds sit in your account, the agent identifies the right moment to sweep them into a yield-bearing product based on your goals and liquidity needs.
- **Account recommendations.** As your financial life evolves, the agent notices when your current account structure no longer fits — and suggests alternatives that serve you better.

<Tip>
  The Account Management agent learns from your own patterns, not a generic profile. The longer you use Buburuza, the more accurate its predictions become.
</Tip>

---

### Payments & Transfers AI

Every payment you make passes through this agent. Its job is to get your money where it needs to go as quickly and cheaply as possible, while stopping fraudulent transactions before they leave your account.

**What it does for you:**

- **Real-time fraud detection.** The agent evaluates every transaction against your behavioral baseline — amount, recipient, timing, device, and location — in under 100 milliseconds. Anomalies trigger additional authentication or a temporary hold.
- **Intelligent routing.** Whether a payment travels over traditional rails (SWIFT, SEPA) or Buburuza-Chain, the agent picks the route with the best combination of speed, cost, and reliability at that moment.
- **Smart fee calculation.** For blockchain transactions, the agent reads network congestion in real time and chooses a gas fee that gets your transaction confirmed promptly without overpaying.

---

### Risk & Compliance AI

Operating across multiple countries means navigating multiple regulatory regimes simultaneously. The Risk & Compliance agent handles this automatically, so you never have to think about it.

**What it does for you:**

- **Continuous transaction monitoring.** The agent screens every transaction for patterns associated with money laundering, sanctions violations, and other financial crimes. When you send money across borders, it applies whichever jurisdiction's rules are most stringent.
- **Reporting support.** The agent assembles and validates the data compliance analysts need for regulatory reporting. Filing decisions are made by qualified staff, not by the model.
- **Market risk assessment.** The agent stress-tests your portfolio exposures against market conditions and flags liquidity concerns before they become problems.

---

### Customer Support AI

No hold music. No business hours. The Customer Support agent is available every hour of every day, across every channel Buburuza operates — web, mobile, and WhatsApp.

**What it does for you:**

- **Multilingual understanding.** The agent processes natural language in 50\+ languages, understands financial terminology, and maintains context across multi-turn conversations. You can describe a problem the way you'd explain it to a friend.
- **Contextual problem-solving.** The agent has access to your account data, transaction history, and product documentation. It can block a card, initiate a dispute, or update your preferences — it doesn't just tell you to go find a form.
- **Intelligent escalation.** When a situation is too complex or when you simply prefer a human, the agent transfers you — along with full context — so you never have to repeat yourself.

<Note>
  The Customer Support agent detects when you're frustrated or distressed and escalates those conversations to priority handling automatically.
</Note>

### Security & Fraud Detection AI

Where the Payments agent focuses on individual transactions, the Security agent watches the bigger picture: how you interact with your account over time.

**What it does for you:**

- **Behavioral fingerprinting.** The agent builds a model of your normal patterns — how you type, how you navigate the app, how long your sessions last, which devices you use. When something deviates from that model, it investigates.
- **Device recognition.** Your trusted devices are identified without storing personally identifiable information. A new, unrecognized device triggers heightened scrutiny.
- **Account takeover prevention.** If risk signals suggest your account may be compromised, the agent can freeze the account, demand additional verification, or contact you through a separate channel — all before an attacker can act.

---

### Business Intelligence AI

The Business Intelligence agent operates entirely on anonymized, aggregated data. It never sees your personal details — its job is to understand how the platform as a whole is performing so Buburuza can improve the product for everyone.

**What it does for you (indirectly):**

- **Better product features.** Usage patterns across the platform reveal where customers get stuck, which features are underused, and where improvements will have the most impact.
- **Proactive retention.** The agent identifies when customers are at risk of disengaging and triggers outreach before that happens — better for you, better for the platform.
- **Relevant recommendations.** When you might benefit from a product upgrade or a new feature, the agent surfaces it at the right moment based on your usage patterns.

## How B1 makes decisions: the suggest-confirm model

B1 is designed to act autonomously on routine tasks and keep you in the loop for anything that matters. The level of automation scales with the stakes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Low-risk operations">
    Actions like checking your balance, retrieving transaction history, or generating a statement execute automatically. No confirmation needed.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Medium-risk operations">
    Small transfers, setting up recurring payments, or enabling a new feature require a single confirmation from you before executing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="High-risk operations">
    Large transfers, account structure changes, or anything the AI flags as unusual requires multi-factor authentication. The system will not proceed without it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When B1's confidence in a decision falls below its internal threshold — or when regulations require human review — the system escalates automatically. You are never at the mercy of a low-confidence AI decision.

## How B1 protects your privacy

AI that learns from your data is only useful if that learning doesn't compromise your privacy. B1 uses several techniques to make these goals compatible:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Differential privacy">
    When B1 learns from aggregate patterns, it applies differential privacy: calibrated noise is added before analysis so that the presence or absence of any one person's data changes the output by a bounded amount. That bound — the privacy budget — is a parameter, not an absolute guarantee. Differential privacy limits inference risk; it does not eliminate it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Federated learning">
    Model improvements happen without your raw data ever leaving your isolated environment. Only anonymized model updates — not your personal transactions — contribute to the shared AI.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Homomorphic encryption">
    In some cases, B1 can compute on your encrypted data without decrypting it first. The result is correct; your underlying data is never exposed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Zero-knowledge proofs">
    When B1 needs to verify something about you — for example, that you meet a certain age requirement — it can confirm the fact cryptographically without revealing the underlying data itself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Isolated processing environments">
    Your data is processed in a dedicated environment isolated from other users' data. Cross-user data leakage is structurally prevented, not just policy-prohibited.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
