## The three problems Buburuza solves

### Problem 1: The missing middle

Traditional banking operates with high overhead costs, slow settlement times, and geographic restrictions. You face lengthy onboarding processes, limited operating hours, and restricted access to advanced financial instruments. DeFi protocols offer 24/7 accessibility and programmable finance—but they present steep learning curves, security risks, and no regulatory protections.

Market research shows that 44% of U.S. Gen Z investors began investing through cryptocurrency, and 55% currently hold crypto assets. Yet current DeFi products remain too complex for mainstream adoption. Buburuza fills that gap: a fully regulated bank account with the speed and programmability of on-chain finance.

### Problem 2: Crypto's stability crisis

The cryptocurrency market lacks stable, transparent, low-risk investment vehicles backed by tangible assets. You currently face a binary choice: speculative tokens promising unrealistic returns, or low-yield DeFi staking with opaque collateral. The tokenized real-world asset market is projected to exceed \$10 trillion by 2030. Buburuza gives you access to that market with proper security, compliance, and monthly cash distributions.

### Problem 3: AI cannot transact

Today's AI systems can analyze financial data, recommend actions, and generate insights—but they cannot independently execute transactions or hold assets. No infrastructure exists for autonomous agents to maintain accounts, make payments, or participate in economic activity. Buburuza builds that infrastructure, designed to authenticate, authorize, and settle transactions between autonomous agents while maintaining full regulatory compliance.

### Your account structure

Every Buburuza account includes:

- **Multi-currency IBANs** — separate account numbers for local and international banking operations
- **Linked blockchain wallets** — non-custodial cryptocurrency wallets with optional institutional-grade custody via Fireblocks
- **Card products** — virtual and physical cards issued through a licensed card issuing partner

### Settlement architecture

Buburuza routes your transactions automatically through the optimal pathway based on cost, speed, and your preferences.

**Traditional rails:**

- SWIFT for international wire transfers (1–3 business days)
- SEPA for European transfers (1 business day)
- Local payment schemes for domestic transfers (same-day to instant)

**Blockchain rails:**

- Buburuza-Chain for internal transfers (sub-second finality)
- Stablecoin transfers via USDB/EURB (instant settlement)
- Cross-chain bridges to Ethereum, Arbitrum, and Base

For example: a $$50 transfer between two Buburuza users settles instantly on-chain with minimal fees. A $$50,000 SWIFT transfer to a traditional bank follows conventional correspondent banking routes with full compliance checks.

## Blockchain infrastructure

### Buburuza-Chain specifications

Buburuza-Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit Layer 3 blockchain optimized for financial applications. As an L3, it settles to Arbitrum (L2), which settles to Ethereum (L1)—giving you Ethereum-level security with dramatically lower costs and faster confirmation.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Block time | 250 milliseconds (4 blocks/second) |
| Gas limit | 15,000,000 per block |
| Throughput | 10,000\+ TPS |
| L3 finality | Sub-second |
| L2 finality | ~15 minutes |
| L1 finality | ~12 minutes |
| Consensus | Proof of Stake with Sequencer model |
| EVM compatibility | Full (standard Solidity smart contracts) |

### Network architecture

Buburuza-Chain uses a semi-decentralized sequencer model that balances performance with censorship resistance:

- **Primary sequencer** — operated by the Buburuza Foundation, processes transactions with minimal latency
- **Backup sequencers** — 5–10 independent operators each bonding a minimum of 100,000 \$BUB
- **Validator network** — expandable via governance, validates sequencer behavior
- **Fraud proofs** — Arbitrum's optimistic rollup security model inherited at L2

Sequencers earn 35% of transaction fees. The remaining fees are allocated to token burns (60%) and the treasury (5%).

### Bridge architecture

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="L2 ↔ L3 bridge">
    Native Arbitrum bridge contracts move assets between Arbitrum (L2) and Buburuza-Chain (L3):

    - **Deposit time:** 1–2 minutes (L2 to L3)
    - **Withdrawal time:** ~15 minutes (L3 to L2, includes challenge period)
    - **Supported assets:** ETH, ERC-20 tokens, NFTs
    - **Security:** Inherits Arbitrum's optimistic rollup guarantees
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="External bridges">
    For assets coming from other chains:

    - **Ethereum:** via Arbitrum's mainnet bridge
    - **Base and Optimism:** via third-party bridge aggregators (Socket, Across)
    - **Bitcoin:** wrapped BTC via BitGo or similar custodians
    - **Other EVM chains:** standard cross-chain messaging protocols

    Bridge fees vary by route. 50–75% of fees are burned, with the remainder going to bridge operators.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Account abstraction

Buburuza implements ERC-4337 account abstraction so you don't need to manage seed phrases or worry about gas tokens:

- **Social recovery** — recover your account through trusted contacts, no seed phrase required
- **Flexible fee payment** — pay gas in \$BUB tokens or approved stablecoins
- **Batch transactions** — bundle multiple operations into a single user action
- **Session keys** — grant temporary permissions to applications without exposing your main keys

## Stablecoin infrastructure

### USDB and EURB

Buburuza's stablecoins are wrapped versions of Circle's USDC and EURC, maintaining 1:1 backing while adding on-chain transparency. Every dollar or euro you deposit is matched by USDC or EURC held in custody.

**How minting works:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    You deposit USDC or EURC through the Buburuza banking interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verification">
    The system confirms your deposit on-chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Minting">
    An equivalent amount of USDB or EURB is minted to your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recording">
    The transaction is recorded on Buburuza-Chain and the total supply updates automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**How redemption works:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request">
    You request to redeem your USDB or EURB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn">
    Your tokens are burned from your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release">
    The equivalent USDC or EURC is released from custody.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer">
    Funds are sent to your specified destination and the total supply decreases automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All stablecoin operations include KYC/AML checks: transaction limits by verification level, velocity checks on large transactions, sanctions screening on all addresses, and suspicious activity reporting.
