# Quickstart

From zero to a working payment account in four steps.

## 1. Sign up

Go to the signup page and create an account with your email:

1. Enter your email and confirm the one-time code.
2. Enroll a passkey (or TOTP if passkeys are unavailable).
3. Save your recovery codes.

## 2. Onboard your organisation

Open the dashboard and complete the onboarding steps:

1. Enter the legal name and company name.
2. Review the KYB document checklist for your jurisdiction.
3. Complete the hosted KYB verification.
4. Wait for the **payment account** to be provisioned (automatic after KYB passes).

<Note>
KYB must pass before money movement is enabled. The dashboard shows the live status.
</Note>

## 3. Fund your account

Once your payment account is **active**:

1. Open **Accounts → your account → Funding**.
2. Pick a funding method:
   - **Fiat** — ACH or wire. You get account and routing details for the selected rail.
   - **Crypto** — a deposit address for your stablecoin wallet.
3. Send funds. Inbound money appears in your transaction list automatically — there is no separate deposit step.

## 4. Send your first transfer

1. Go to **Transfers → New transfer**.
2. Pick the source wallet, destination, and amount.
3. Review the **fee preview** — the fee is shown before you confirm and is charged on top of the amount (the recipient receives exactly the amount you entered).
4. Confirm. Track the transfer in **Transactions**.
