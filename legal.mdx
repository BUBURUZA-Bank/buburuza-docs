This page states plainly what Buburuza is, who holds which regulatory permission, and how your personal data is handled. If anything elsewhere in this documentation appears to conflict with this page, this page governs.

## What Buburuza is

Buburuza is a financial technology company. It is **not a bank**, and it does not hold a banking licence in any jurisdiction.

<Warning>
  Buburuza does not take deposits and does not offer deposit insurance. Balances are not insured by the FDIC, CDIC, or any other deposit insurance scheme. Funds are held with regulated payment and custody partners, and are subject to those partners' terms.
</Warning>

## Who holds which permission

Regulated money movement is performed by licensed partners who act as the money transmitter of record for the corridor. Buburuza operates as a technology and compliance layer on top of them.

| Jurisdiction | Who holds the permission | Buburuza's role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | Licensed partner — money transmitter of record, files in its own name | Transaction monitoring and customer risk scoring |
| Canada | FINTRAC Money Services Business registration — application in progress | Transaction monitoring and customer risk scoring; regulatory reporting once registered |

Features are enabled market by market as permissions are granted. Where Buburuza and its partners do not hold the required permission, the feature is not offered in that market.

## Products that are not offered

Several capabilities described elsewhere in this documentation are planned rather than available. The following are **not offered today** and carry their own licensing conditions before they could be:

- Interest or yield on balances
- Insurance products
- Lending, credit lines, and credit cards
- Investment advice and discretionary portfolio management
- Tokenized real-world asset investments

Pages describing planned capabilities carry a banner saying so.

## Securities

Tokenized real-world assets and the \$BUB token are not offered or sold through this documentation. Nothing here is an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security, or a recommendation to enter any transaction. Where such instruments are offered, they are subject to offering documents, eligibility rules, and risk disclosure, and availability differs by jurisdiction and by investor status.

## Advice

Buburuza does not provide legal, tax, or investment advice. Information in this documentation is general and does not take your circumstances into account. Where a trust or similar structure is referenced, its effect depends on your own residency and circumstances, and it can create reporting obligations — for US persons, IRS Forms 3520 and 3520-A among them. Speak to your own adviser.

## Your personal data

Buburuza processes personal data to open and operate your account, to meet legal obligations, and to prevent fraud and financial crime.

| Category | Examples | Why it is processed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Name, date of birth, identity document, liveness check | Legal obligation — customer due diligence |
| Contact | Email, phone, address | Contract performance and service notices |
| Transaction | Payments, counterparties, balances | Contract performance and legal obligation |
| Device and session | Device identifiers, IP address, session activity | Legitimate interest — fraud prevention and account security |

**Your rights.** Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, ask for its deletion, object to certain processing, or receive a copy in portable form. To exercise any of these, contact [privacy@buburuza.com](mailto:privacy@buburuza.com).

**Retention.** Records required by anti-money-laundering law are retained for the period the applicable law requires, which is generally five years after the account closes or the transaction completes. Other data is kept only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for.

**Automated decisions.** Where an automated system materially affects a decision about you, you can ask for the decision to be reviewed by a person. Contact support or the address above.

## The agreements that govern your account

Three documents govern your relationship with Buburuza. You accept them at signup, and the current version of each is available in the app under **Settings → Documents**:

- **Terms and Conditions**
- **Privacy Policy**
- **Electronic Communications Agreement**

Each is versioned. When a document changes, you are notified and asked to accept the new version within the grace period stated in the notice.

## Complaints

If something has gone wrong, contact [support@buburuza.com](mailto:support@buburuza.com) with your account details and what happened. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may be able to escalate to the regulator or ombudsman service in your jurisdiction; the response to your complaint will tell you which applies to you.
