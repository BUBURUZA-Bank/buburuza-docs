## Know which payment fields serve which purpose

| Field | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Recipient gets | The amount and currency intended for the recipient. |
| You pay / Total debited | The source-side amount, including the fee or conversion breakdown shown. |
| Name to display / Memo / Message to beneficiary | The description associated with the outgoing payment. |
| Email receipt to | The address selected for the payment receipt. |
| Include attachments | Whether to include supporting material with the receipt when that option appears. |
| Invoice number, invoice date, due date | Reference information associated with the invoice. |
| Internal note | The web form describes this as visible to people with access to the Buburuza account. |
| Attachments | Supporting files associated with the payment. |

Review the receipt destination and attachment choice separately from your internal note. These controls address different audiences. After authorizing, use the payment record and its reference to track the result.

## Send an ACH payment

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Choose ACH when it is offered for the bank payment you want to make. Review the recipient, funding account, amount, and displayed fee before confirming.

1. Open Send money, choose the recipient, and select ACH from the saved or available payment methods.
2. Review the recipient bank details. If you edit pre-filled details on the web, decide whether to save the changes to the recipient or use them for this payment only.
3. Choose Sending from on mobile or Send from on the web, then enter Recipient gets.
4. Complete Name to display on the payment and the optional memo and receipt-email information. The web flow also includes invoice number, invoice date, due date, an internal note, and attachments.
5. Read Review Payment on mobile or Review on the web. Check the recipient, account and routing information, funding account, fee, and total debit.
6. On mobile, use Slide to send. On the web, choose Send payment and complete the passkey challenge shown.
7. Read the payment result. On the web, the result includes Payment ID and Receipt sent to. Open Transactions to inspect the ongoing status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the balance is insufficient, check the available balance and total debit rather than just the amount you typed.
- If confirmation is interrupted, check Transactions before starting the payment again to avoid a duplicate.

## Send a domestic wire payment

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Use the wire option shown for your recipient and account. Domestic-wire details can differ from ACH details, so confirm the payment method before sending.

1. Open Send money, select the recipient, and choose Wire.
2. Review or add the wire routing number, bank, account number, beneficiary name, and beneficiary address. Verify wire instructions rather than assuming the ACH details are interchangeable.
3. Choose the funding account and enter the amount the recipient should receive.
4. Complete the memo or Message to beneficiary and any receipt, invoice, internal-note, or attachment fields needed for your records.
5. On Review, compare Recipient gets, Wire fee, Total debited, recipient details, and the displayed arrival estimate.
6. On mobile, use Slide to send. On the web, choose Send payment and complete the passkey challenge shown.
7. Read the result and open the transaction record. Keep its Payment ID or bank reference when you need help.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the recipient’s instructions do not match the details on your screen, stop and verify them before confirming.
- If a wire remains pending or shows a failure, use the transaction reference when contacting support. Do not assume you can cancel or recall it from the recipient list.

## Send an international wire

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

An international-wire review may show both what you pay and what the recipient receives. Check the currencies, bank details, conversion information, and fee treatment together.

1. Select the recipient and International wire.
2. Review the SWIFT code, IBAN, bank name, beneficiary name, and beneficiary address. If details were scanned from an invoice, compare every field with the source document.
3. If the app shows a SWIFT/IBAN mismatch, verify the bank details with the recipient. Use the suggested-code control only after checking that the suggested bank is correct.
4. Choose the funding account and currencies. Enter You pay or Recipient gets and inspect the corresponding amount.
5. Review the exchange rate, conversion fee, wire fee, and total debit. Inspect Cover the fees or Cover all transfer fees and the explanation shown before deciding whether to enable it.
6. Complete Who or what is this payment for? and the payment name or memo. In the additional-review panel, describe the work or goods; the recipient website or LinkedIn and supporting documents are optional fields.
7. On the web, review receipt email, invoice details, internal note, attachments, and any displayed send-date or repeat controls.
8. If you edited recipient wire details, choose whether to save them for future payments or use them only for this payment.
9. Review the complete payment and authorize using the displayed mobile or web control. Keep the Payment ID and tracking link shown in the result.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If Buburuza flags a mismatch between bank identifiers, verify the details with the recipient before accepting a suggested correction.
- If the final amount differs from your expectation, return to the quote and fee details before sending.

## Review fees before confirming a payment

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Review the fee breakdown on the payment screen before authorizing the transaction. The screen may show a transfer fee, network fee, or conversion information depending on the method.

1. Choose the payment method, funding source, and amount.
2. Open or read the fee and conversion details on the review screen.
3. Compare the amount sent, total deducted, and any displayed recipient amount.
4. If you change the amount, currency, or method, review the updated total again.
5. After submission, open the transaction record to see the information retained for that payment.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The FX-rate comparison page is not a substitute for the quote on your payment review screen.
- If you need to question a fee, give support the transaction reference and the line item you want explained.

## Check a payment’s status

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

The transaction record is the place to check what happened after you submitted a payment. A confirmation screen, a pending entry, and a completed transaction are different states.

1. Open Transactions or recent activity for the relevant account or wallet, then open the payment.
2. Read the status and Status timeline. Compare the initiated, processing, confirmed, completed, rejected, or returned events that appear for that payment.
3. For a crypto transfer, read the network status as well as the account status. Confirmed on-chain, posting to your account and Awaiting account posting explain why a transfer can remain Pending after network confirmation.
4. For a bank transfer, inspect the payment method and any bank reason or processing explanation shown.
5. Use the reference appropriate to the method when asking for help: ACH trace number, Fed reference (IMAD), SWIFT UETR, or crypto transaction hash, when available.
6. Review the fee and total-debit information on the same record. A tracking or receipt page can supplement the record, but does not replace checking the current account status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If you cannot find the payment, check the selected account and any active filters.
- If you are unsure whether a payment was submitted, check activity before creating another one.

## Read a payment tracking link

_Applies to: Web; shared tracking page_

An international-payment confirmation can include a tracking link. Its page describes the payment’s progress and provides a way for the recipient to follow it.

1. Open the tracking link supplied with the payment confirmation.
2. Check the sender and payment details shown before relying on the status.
3. If the status is Scheduled, read the planned send date and any explanation on the page.
4. If the status is In transit, read the current stage, such as an intermediary-bank step, and the payment-specific arrival estimate.
5. If the status is Delivered, read the delivery explanation and check the recipient account for the actual credit.
6. If the link has expired, ask the sender for an updated confirmation. Use the sender or Buburuza support route shown on the page for questions.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A scheduled payment has not necessarily been sent yet.
- A tracking link and a payment-request link serve different purposes: one reports an outgoing payment, while the other asks someone to pay.
- If the tracking link has expired or no longer opens, ask the sender for current tracking information.

## Send an ACH payment from the mobile bank-details form

_Applies to: Mobile_

The mobile ACH form collects the receiving bank and address, then lets you fund the dollar payment from an offered fiat account or crypto asset. The recipient receives the dollar amount shown on review.

1. Open Payments and choose ACH. In Sending from, select the fiat account or wallet asset you intend to use. Select many opens the separate multiple-account selection.
2. Enter or paste the routing number, inspect the matching bank, then select it. Confirm that it matches the recipient’s ACH instructions.
3. Open Account type, select Personal or Business, then Checking or Savings. Enter or paste the account number.
4. Open Recipient’s address. Enter the street address, city, state and ZIP code. If an address suggestion fills these fields, check each field before choosing Done.
5. When the bank and address fields are complete, choose Continue. Check the funding account or asset and its available balance.
6. Enter an amount or use an offered percentage or Max control. ACH is presented in USD; when funding from another currency or crypto, review the source amount, equivalent and Recipient gets. Use the unit-switch control if you want to enter the other displayed unit.
7. On Review, check From, routing number, bank, account type, account number, recipient address, source Amount and Recipient gets.
8. Check Pay fee with and the offered timing/fee choice, then use the Send slider when the details match your intended payment.
9. Check the result record. Use Check details or Track for the existing transfer; Receipt and Attach are separate record actions. An Internal note is described as private to you.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Continue is unavailable while required bank/address fields or the amount are incomplete.
- Insufficient funds appears in red when the entered amount exceeds the selected balance. Reduce the amount or review the available funding sources before continuing.
- A bank match identifies the bank; it does not confirm that the account number belongs to the intended recipient.
- Read the current offered timing and fee before sending. A result screen is not proof that the receiving bank has posted the money.

## Send a wire from the mobile bank-details form

_Applies to: Mobile_

The mobile Wire flow separates the source of funds, the destination bank details and the sending currency. Check all three before authorizing the payment.

1. Open Payments and choose Wire. Choose a source in Sending from, or Select many for the multiple-account route.
2. Enter or paste the routing number and check the bank match against the recipient’s wire instructions.
3. Choose Account type using Personal or Business and Checking or Savings. Enter the account number and recipient’s address, then continue when the required details are complete.
4. On the amount screen, check the source account or asset. Open the currency control to inspect Sending currency, then select the available currency you intend to send.
5. Enter an amount or use Max. If the source uses a different currency or is a crypto asset, compare the debit amount, equivalent and Recipient gets. The circular control switches the amount-entry unit.
6. On Review, check From, routing number, bank, account type, account number, address, source Amount and Recipient gets.
7. Read Pay fee with, Fee and Arrives in. Use the Send slider after checking the complete payment.
8. Open the result’s Check details or Track action to inspect the transfer. Use Receipt, Attach or the private Internal note for your records.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If Insufficient funds is displayed, reduce the amount or change the selected funding before reviewing the payment again.
- The sending-currency list is separate from the source account’s currency. Confirm Recipient gets after changing either one.
- Confirm wire instructions with the recipient if the bank details do not match; do not infer that ACH and wire instructions are interchangeable.

## Complete the mobile international-wire form

_Applies to: Mobile_

International wire asks for destination-specific bank details, the recipient’s address and the payment purpose before you review currency, charges and the amount.

1. Open Payments and choose Inter-wire or International wire. Select the funding account or wallet in Sending from.
2. Enter or paste the SWIFT code and inspect the matching bank. Complete the account fields presented for that bank. The form may ask for IBAN, or for Account number and a local bank identifier such as IFSC.
3. Choose Add recipient’s address. Complete Legal address, Country, City, State/Province/Region and Postal/ZIP code. Apt/Suite/Floor is optional. Check any address suggestion before selecting Add address.
4. Add Recipient’s phone number if needed, using the correct country dial code. This field is optional in the form.
5. Choose who will receive the funds, then describe what the payment is for. The list includes Employee, Contractor, Vendor, Landlord, Transferring to subsidiary and Other. If a Purpose code applies, search its description and select the appropriate offered code.
6. Complete the optional memo, supporting documents, receipt email and internal note as needed, then Continue. Review attachment-sharing separately from storing an attachment.
7. Choose the sending currency and enter the amount. Check source currency or asset, equivalent, and Recipient gets. Use the unit control to change the entry unit when offered.
8. Review the destination bank and address, payment relationship/purpose, memo, files, email recipients, internal note and funding source.
9. Compare Send as SHA and Send as OUR using the explanation shown. Review Pay fee with, the fee, the sender debit and recipient amount before using the Send slider.
10. Check the result and open Check details or Track for the existing transfer. Keep the payment reference when requesting help.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Bank fields can change after the SWIFT bank is selected. Complete the fields displayed for that destination rather than forcing an IBAN into an account-number field.
- An optional purpose code can be left empty when the required relationship and description are complete. If you enter a code, check that it accurately describes this payment.
- Recheck currency and amounts after switching source, sending currency or entry unit.
- If a bank identifier, amount or charge explanation is unclear, resolve it before authorizing the payment.

## Add notes, documents and receipt recipients to an international wire

_Applies to: Mobile_

A payment memo, a supporting document, an emailed receipt and an internal note have different audiences. Check each field before sending the international wire.

1. At the optional-information step, enter Memo only for text you intend the recipient to see with the payment.
2. Choose Upload under Attachments. Select an offered method: Scan using camera, Upload from photo library, Choose from files, Forward by email or Enter manually.
3. Check the attached-file row. Its overflow menu offers View and Delete; inspect the file before continuing.
4. Under Email receipt to, choose Add recipient, enter the email address and choose Add recipient in the sheet. Use X to remove an unintended address.
5. Select Send the attached files to the recipient by email only when those files should be included. Storing a file on the payment and sharing it by email are separate choices.
6. Use Internal note for information the app describes as internal. Continue to Review and check memo, attachments, receipt recipients and the sharing selection together.
7. After submission, inspect the existing payment record. The internal note is retained in the shown result.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Adding a receipt email does not itself confirm that the receipt or documents were delivered.
- An attachment row does not prove its contents were read, validated or shared.
- Follow the file-type and size requirements displayed by the upload step. Check the attached filename before continuing.

## Compare international-wire charge options

_Applies to: Mobile_

The international-wire review offers different ways to handle transfer charges. Compare the explanation, fee and amount shown before selecting an option.

1. Open the international-wire Review and locate Send as SHA and Send as OUR, or the fee-covering control shown in your flow.
2. Read SHA — Shared Charges. It explains that you pay the app fee while intermediary or recipient banks may deduct their fees from the transferred amount.
3. Read OUR — Sender Pays All. It describes covering the transfer charges so the intended recipient amount is preserved. Check the terms and any route-specific conditions presented for your actual transfer.
4. Compare Pay fee with, Fee, You send or Recipient gets, and the final amount on the send control after changing the selection.
5. Authorize only when the selected charge option and displayed debit match your intention, then retain the transaction reference.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Changing a charge option can change the fee and total. Recheck them before sending.
- Do not use an example fee or exchange rate from another payment to calculate the current debit.
- If the fee or recipient-amount explanation does not match the displayed figures, resolve the discrepancy before authorizing.

## Review funding and charges on the web

_Applies to: Web_

The web payment forms described here can fund bank or crypto transfers from selected fiat or crypto balances. Keep the funding source, outgoing amount and fee source distinct.

1. Choose the payment rail, then use Sending from to choose the account or wallet funding it. Where Select many is offered, select sources and review their order before Continue with multiple accounts.
2. For ACH, complete routing number, detected bank, account type, account number and recipient address. The amount screen describes sending USD and converting a non-USD funding balance when needed.
3. For International wire, complete SWIFT and the bank-specific account fields, recipient address and optional phone. Review relationship, payment description and any applicable purpose code.
4. Keep the recipient-visible Memo separate from Internal note. If emailing a receipt, review its recipients and the separate Send the attached files to the recipient by email checkbox.
5. Enter the amount, using Max or the unit switch if needed. Compare the source debit with the destination currency or token amount.
6. On bank review, inspect Pay fee with, timing, full recipient data and total. For International wire, review Send as SHA or Send as OUR and the displayed charge allocation.
7. Choose Confirm and send only after the bank-payment review is correct. For crypto, check the outgoing token/network, address and fee/speed option before choosing Send on the review.
8. Check the existing payment’s result and use Receipt, Track, Check details, Attach or Copy link for the record.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If Insufficient funds appears and Continue is disabled, adjust the funding or amount before proceeding.
- Read the SHA/OUR terms, fees and timing quoted for this transfer before sending.
- Team-visible notes in a business view differ from the personal-only note wording in other layouts. Read the visibility helper where you enter the note.
- Review the complete payment summary even when details have been prefilled or reused from an earlier step.
