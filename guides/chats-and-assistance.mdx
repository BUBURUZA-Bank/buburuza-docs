## Use chats and review payment requests

_Applies to: Mobile_

Chats brings together personal conversations, Support, Concierge and B1. Payment cards appear alongside messages, while sending money uses a separate review step.

1. Open Chats from the bottom navigation. Choose B1, Support or Concierge at the top, or open a conversation from the list. Search helps you locate a conversation.
2. Read the conversation and enter a reply in the message field. Use the send arrow to send text.
3. To dictate a message, use the microphone. While speech is being captured, the composer shows a waveform, Cancel and a checkmark. Review the resulting text before sending it.
4. The personal-chat composer also offers Request, Transfer, Swap and More. Choose Transfer to enter the amount and continue to its separate review screen.
5. Read the label on a payment card. An incoming amount, an outgoing Transfer and a Payment request are different entries. Verify the amount, person and purpose before taking payment action.
6. For help with a payment, check the transaction record and open Support from Chats.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A typed message saying that money was sent does not confirm a transfer. Check the transaction record.
- If dictated text is wrong, correct it before sending or use Cancel while recording.
- If an unexpected payment request arrives, verify it before paying. Never share security codes or wallet recovery information in chat.

## Review a concierge request and payment

_Applies to: Mobile_

Concierge lets you discuss a service, review an itemized offer and authorize its payment separately. Keep the service details and payment request together so you can check what you have agreed to.

1. Open Chats, then Concierge. Open an existing topic or choose New chat. Suggested requests include Flight ticket, Book a hotel, Shopping, Book a table and Car rent.
2. Describe what you need. For a flight request, provide departure and arrival points and dates, the number of travelers and child ages, cabin and seat preferences, and any additional services or frequent-flyer details requested.
3. Review the proposed option. Check travel dates, departure and arrival times, airports, airline, cabin and price. Reply with any changes before agreeing.
4. If you want a hotel or airport transfer, discuss those details in the same conversation. Check the hotel dates, room, included services and price, and the transfer route and price.
5. Review the Final Booking Offer. Check every service and the total. Ask for any missing information or correction, then confirm the arrangement in the conversation when you agree.
6. Open the Payment request card. Choose Pay to continue, or Decline request if you do not accept it.
7. On Payment, check Service and Amount, then select the funding source under From. Review the completed payment summary and swipe Pay to authorize it.
8. Check the request card for Paid and the concierge reply. Keep the conversation, and check the separate supplier booking confirmation for the travel or service itself.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If Pay is inactive and From still says Select, choose the funding source before continuing.
- If the description, dates or total do not match what you agreed, ask the concierge to correct the request before paying.
- A Paid card and a completed conversation confirm different things from an airline ticket, hotel reservation or supplier booking. Obtain and check the service confirmation.
- For changes, cancellation or a payment problem, continue the relevant concierge conversation. The applicable supplier terms determine what can be changed or refunded.

## Ask B1 about your account activity

_Applies to: Mobile_

B1 is an assistant conversation for questions about account activity. You can type or speak a request. If a request leads to a transfer, review and authorize that transfer separately.

1. Open Chats and choose B1, or use the floating B1 icon on Home.
2. Type a question or choose a suggestion such as How is my spending this month? The start screen also offers a quarterly-income question, Analyse my docs and More.
3. Read the response. A spending summary can explain the total, comparison with average spending, changes by category, recurring bills and progress toward a savings goal. Check the account records when you need to confirm a figure.
4. To speak, use the voice control. Cancel is available while voice capture is active. Check that the transcript says what you intended.
5. Use the conversation icon to return to B1 topics. The list shows named conversations, timestamps and unread counts; the minimized bar keeps the current conversation accessible while you return to Home.
6. If you ask B1 to prepare a transfer, answer its follow-up questions about the payment method and account. Check Transfer to Bubu user when paying a Buburuza contact.
7. On the separate Review screen, check Rail, From, To, Amount and the displayed arrival estimate. Swipe Send only when you want to authorize the transfer.
8. Check the resulting transaction state in your account. Use Support if the request or payment needs help.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A request in the conversation does not mean a transfer has been made. Review the separate authorization screen and check the transaction afterward.
- If a recipient, rail, account or amount is wrong, correct the request before authorizing it.
- Do not enter passwords, verification codes, wallet recovery information or private keys into B1.
- If an answer does not resolve an account problem, use Support.

## Send money from a personal chat

_Applies to: Mobile_

You can prepare a transfer from a personal conversation and add an optional message or illustration before reviewing it.

1. Open Chats and choose the person you want to pay. Check that you have the right conversation.
2. Open the message composer and choose Transfer.
3. Choose the source account, check the available balance and currency, and enter the amount. Continue when the details are correct.
4. Add a message if you want to explain the payment. Use the emoji control to open Choose an emoji, select the illustration and choose Set. You can use Skip if you do not want to add anything.
5. Check the message and illustration preview, then choose Continue.
6. On Review, check From, To, Amount, the attached message and the displayed arrival estimate. Swipe Send to authorize the transfer.
7. Return to the conversation to see the Transfer card. Check the transaction record for its current status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the person, account, currency or amount is wrong, correct the details before using Send.
- An optional message or illustration does not change the transfer amount.
- If a transfer appears wrong or has not arrived, check its transaction status and contact Support. Do not rely on a chat action to reverse a completed payment.

## Start a support chat and send attachments

_Applies to: Mobile_

Support in Chats keeps your conversation and helpful attachments together. You can return to an existing topic or start a new one.

1. Open Chats and choose Support.
2. Open an existing topic, or choose New chat. Existing conversations show their topic and recent message preview.
3. Choose the closest category: Card issue, Account issue, Security issue, Multisig issue or Transaction issue. Use More if your topic is not listed, or describe the problem in the message field.
4. Explain what you were trying to do, what happened and the error you saw. Reply to the support team in the same conversation.
5. To attach a screenshot or file, choose the plus control. In Upload document, choose Upload from photo library or Choose from files.
6. Check the staged thumbnails before sending. Remove an unwanted attachment with its x control, then use the send arrow when the selection is correct.
7. Confirm that the attachments appear as a message in the conversation. You can reopen the topic from the Support list to continue.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Thumbnails above the composer are staged attachments. Check that they appear in the conversation after sending.
- Remove private information that is not needed to explain the issue. Never send passwords, app passcodes, one-time codes, recovery codes, private keys, seed phrases or full payment-card details.
- For identity or financial documents, follow the secure upload instructions provided for your specific case.
- If an attachment does not appear, tell support what happened in the same conversation and include the error text if one appears.

## Manage archived chats

_Applies to: Mobile_

The Archive list lets you select conversations to unarchive or delete. Deletion has a separate confirmation and is described as irreversible.

1. Open the Archive list in Chats and find the conversation you want to manage. Use its search control if needed.
2. Select the conversations using their checkboxes, or choose Select all.
3. To return selected conversations from the archive, choose Unarchive.
4. To remove selected conversations, choose Delete. Check the number of selected chats in the confirmation.
5. Choose No, cancel to keep them, or Yes, delete chats to confirm. The completion state shows Chats deleted and the remaining conversations.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The confirmation warns that deleted chats cannot be recovered. Check the selection before confirming.
- If you only want to take a conversation out of the archive, use Unarchive.
- Deleting a conversation is not a payment-cancellation action. Check the transaction record separately for payment issues.

## Review payment details filled by B1

_Applies to: Mobile_

B1 can offer contextual autofill while you prepare an ACH transfer. Review every populated payment detail before continuing.

1. Open the ACH recipient form. If B1 offers Autofill with a recent routing number, choose the intended suggestion or open the B1 conversation.
2. When B1 asks which recent routing number to use, select the correct number chip or reply with the intended selection.
3. Wait for the assistant and form-loading indicators to finish.
4. Compare Routing number, detected Bank, Account type, Account number and Recipient’s address against the recipient’s confirmed information. Use the form’s clear/edit controls for anything incorrect.
5. Choose Continue only after the details are correct, then review and authorize the payment through the normal ACH flow.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Autofill does not authorize or submit a payment.
- A recent routing-number suggestion does not prove that the other populated account or address details belong to the intended recipient.
- About ACH transfer opens assistance; it is different from choosing a destination or sending money.

## Use the desktop B1 assistant panel

_Applies to: Web_

The desktop B1 layout supports a floating conversation, a minimized header and a docked side panel. You can type or speak a question and return to the current conversation.

1. Choose the floating B1 icon on Home. Select a suggested prompt or open B1 chat.
2. Enter your question in Enter message. Suggestions include monthly spending, quarterly net income, Analyse my docs and More.
3. Use the microphone or voice control to speak. Check the provisional text and choose Cancel if you want to stop the visible recording state.
4. Read the response and compare financial figures with the corresponding account records.
5. Use the minimize control to fold the conversation to its header. The floating panel can be moved away from its side dock or expanded into the full right panel.
6. Use the conversation controls to resume it, or X to close the panel.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- An answer or spoken transfer request does not establish that a payment was submitted.
- Choosing Analyse my docs or an alert suggestion starts a request. Check the response or saved alert before treating the task as complete.
- If a request involves a payment, review and authorize it through the payment flow actually offered.

## Organize concierge requests and review event cards

_Applies to: Mobile_

The concierge conversation list separates General chat from conversations for individual requests. Keep the preferences, replies and event information for each request together.

1. Open Concierge in Chats. Use General chat for general questions, or select the named request conversation you want to continue.
2. To start a request, choose the plus control. Select the relevant request: I need flight ticket, Book a hotel, I need to buy something, I want to travel, I want to rent a car, Book me a table, I need an errand or I am looking for exciting events.
3. Enter the requested preferences. For a flight, these can include departure and arrival points, dates, traveller count and child ages, cabin or seating preferences, extra services and frequent-flyer details.
4. Review your message before sending it. Read the manager’s follow-up questions and keep your replies in the same request conversation.
5. If an Event card appears, check its title, dates, location and attachment count. Use View to inspect the information offered for that event.
6. Check any Added to calendar status on the card. If you need the event in another calendar, verify it in that calendar separately.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A completed conversation or manager confirmation does not by itself show payment or an issued ticket. Review the separate payment request and the booking records supplied for your request.
- Added to calendar in the conversation does not confirm an export or synchronization to a personal calendar.
- If event details or attachments are missing, ask the concierge in that request conversation before relying on the itinerary.
