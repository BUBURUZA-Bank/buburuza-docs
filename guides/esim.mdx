## Choose and purchase an eSIM data plan

_Applies to: Mobile_

Choose a destination and data plan, review how you will pay, then check the purchase result. Buying a plan and installing an eSIM are separate steps.

1. Open eSIM and choose Get eSIM for your first eSIM, or + New eSIM when you already have one.
2. In Explore plans, search for your destination or choose it from Top picks or All destinations.
3. Review the allowance, duration and price. If Unlimited GB is offered, select the number of days shown by that plan. Choose Continue.
4. In Purchase eSIM, choose Your Buburuza to select an offered account or card, or External to use an available external payment method.
5. For an external card, choose a saved card or New card. A new-card form asks for card number, expiration date and CVV and lets you choose whether to save the card for future topups.
6. On Review, check Country of use, Plan, Type, Duration, Total amount and Payment method. Correct anything that does not match your trip or intended payment.
7. Use the final Buy or Continue control shown for the selected method and complete any payment prompts provided. Check the result: Processing your payment is separate from the ready screen.
8. When the eSIM is ready, choose Install or Install later. Return to the eSIM overview to check its status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Use an enabled payment method offered to you. A method labelled Coming soon cannot be selected for payment.
- If payment is processing, check that existing purchase before starting another one.
- Review the plan details for supported destinations, duration, data use and price. Check fair-use, speed and renewal conditions even when a plan is labelled Unlimited.
- Purchase readiness does not mean the eSIM is installed or the plan is activated.

## My eSIM says Not installed

_Applies to: Mobile_

An eSIM can be purchased while its overview still says Not installed. Installation, plan activation and remaining data are separate statuses.

1. Open eSIM and select the eSIM card you want to install. If you have several, swipe to the intended card.
2. Check its installation status and the plan listed underneath it.
3. Choose Install on the ready screen or on a Not installed card. If you previously chose Install later, return to that card to continue.
4. Follow the phone installation instructions actually presented by the app and your device.
5. Return to the overview and check for Installed. Review the separate plan status and data information; an Installed label alone does not establish that the plan has activated or connected.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Add data purchases a plan or additional data; it is not a substitute for installing the eSIM.
- Follow the current provider instructions for your device’s installation, activation, roaming and connection settings.
- Do not delete an eSIM as a routine installation fix. The removal confirmation explicitly warns that active plans will be removed.
- If installation fails, record the displayed error and device details for support before retrying or removing the eSIM.

## Check eSIM data, add a plan, or manage an eSIM

_Applies to: Mobile_

The eSIM overview shows the selected eSIM, its installation status, and its plans. Check the selected card before adding data or opening Settings.

1. Open eSIM. Swipe the card carousel if you have more than one eSIM and select the intended card.
2. Read the plan country, validity date, remaining allowance and usage percentage where shown. Not activated is a separate plan state.
3. To buy more data, use Add data or the plus beside a plan. Review the destination, allowance, duration and payment before confirming the purchase.
4. Use All plans where it appears to open the available plan view.
5. Open Settings for the eSIM management choices. Reinstall leads to the device installation flow; follow its current instructions.
6. If you choose removal, read the Active plans will be removed warning. Choose Close to keep the eSIM, or Delete anyway only when you intend that removal.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Check which eSIM is selected before purchasing a plan; multiple cards can have different destinations and installation states.
- Check the plan terms for the activation event and when the validity period begins.
- Treat removal as a separate action from pausing a plan. Confirm its effect on access, remaining data and any refund before proceeding; do not use it as an assumed connectivity fix.

## Customize an eSIM plan and choose its payment method

_Applies to: Mobile_

Choose the data allowance and duration for a custom eSIM plan, then check the plan and payment method together before purchasing.

1. Open the eSIM destination list and select a country. If Custom plan is available, select it instead of a preset under Available plans.
2. Use the separate minus and plus controls to adjust the data allowance and number of days. Check Total amount, then select Continue.
3. At Checkout, review the country, provider, data allowance, duration and price. Use Edit plan if these do not match your selection.
4. Open Payment method and select an available saved card or Apple Pay. To add a card, select Add new card and enter its card number, expiry date and CVV, then select Add when enabled.
5. Check that the selected method appears at Checkout. Follow the payment control and any authorization instructions presented for that method.
6. After eSIM added successfully, choose Explore my eSIMs to review your eSIMs or Install eSIM to begin installation. Check the installation status separately.
7. If you need to manage saved methods, open Payment method and select Edit. Review the method beside each removal control carefully, and select Done when you have finished.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If Custom plan is unavailable for a destination, choose from the available plans and review their allowances and duration.
- If Add remains unavailable, check the card number, expiry date and CVV fields. Do not send these details to support.
- Payment processing, a purchased eSIM, an installed eSIM and an active data plan are different states. Check the current eSIM and plan status before relying on mobile data.
- Before removing a saved method or plan, read any confirmation displayed. Removing a saved payment method does not by itself establish a refund or cancellation of an eSIM plan.
