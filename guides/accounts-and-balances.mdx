## Read the account, asset, and network together

A bank account has a currency, account holder, and bank details. A crypto holding also has an asset and network. The combined web Accounts view can display a token total before showing its separate network balances.

Before sending or receiving crypto, open the network row you intend to use. Check the asset, selected network, and address on the transaction screen. A token name or a combined balance alone does not identify the right receiving instructions.

Home’s display currency and chart period change how you view balances. They are not exchange instructions. On the web, Balance details separates Current balance, Pending outgoing, Available balance, and Pending deposits; use the amount and funding source shown in the payment itself before authorizing spending.

## Price comparison, balance and transaction quote

A token’s price chart, your token balance and a transaction quote describe different things. The chart has selectable periods. **My balance** shows the token quantity, network and converted fiat value. A transfer or swap review shows the particular amount and fees for that transaction.

The **FX Rate** calculator compares a base amount across selected currencies. Adding, removing or reordering currencies changes the comparison. It does not exchange funds. Check Last updated and use the transaction’s own review when you intend to move money.

## Understand your home screen and balances

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Home on mobile and Dashboard on the web bring together balances, recent activity, and account actions. Review the balance label as well as the number: a total or chart value is not necessarily the amount available to spend.

1. Check the selected company or profile before using a balance or transaction.
2. Review the verification banner. Verify begins the business-verification journey; Finish verifying resumes incomplete information; Verifying or the review message identifies a submitted review.
3. On mobile, inspect Total wealth, Accounts & wallets, and Transactions. Use the chart periods such as 1D, 1W, 1M, 6M, 1Y, or 5Y to change the displayed period.
4. On the web, open Balance details to inspect Current balance, Pending outgoing, Available balance, and Pending deposits separately.
5. Use the web period menu for recent-day ranges or week, month, quarter, and year to date. Use the currency selector to choose a display unit.
6. Open the specific account or wallet for its own balance and activity. Use View all or the transaction list to inspect an individual payment.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If a chart value differs from the amount available for a payment, check the individual account balance and any pending activity.
- Changing the chart period or display currency changes the view; it does not itself convert money.

## Switch between your account profiles

_Applies to: Mobile_

Use the account switcher to choose the profile you want to work in. Your profile list can include business, personal, or family accounts; the choices shown in your account depend on what you have access to.

1. Open the current account or profile name from the home screen.
2. Choose the account you want from the list.
3. Wait for the switch to complete.
4. Check the displayed account name and balances before starting a transfer, opening documents, or changing settings.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If an expected account is missing, confirm that you signed in with the correct identity and contact support about access.
- Switching profiles does not transfer money between them.

## Find your bank account details

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Open the relevant bank account to find the receiving details provided for it. Use the details for the correct currency and transfer method.

1. Open Accounts & wallets on mobile or Accounts on the web.
2. Choose the bank account that should receive the money.
3. Open its account details or receiving details.
4. Check the account holder, bank details, and the transfer method shown.
5. Use the available copy or share control to pass the receiving instructions to the sender.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If details are unavailable, check whether verification is still outstanding.
- Do not reuse details from a different account or assume domestic and international instructions are interchangeable.

## Understand wallets, currencies, and networks

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Bank accounts carry an account currency. Crypto holdings also have an asset and a network. In the combined Accounts view, a token can appear as an aggregate with a separate balance for each network.

1. Open Accounts & wallets on mobile, or the relevant Accounts or Wallets view on the web.
2. For a bank account, check its account name, currency, and masked account ending.
3. For a crypto holding, open the token or wallet and inspect the network rows. The combined web view shows a network count before you open those rows.
4. Read the asset quantity separately from its displayed currency equivalent.
5. Select the relevant network before using Send or Receive, then inspect the address and next-screen instructions.
6. Use the account or wallet activity list to inspect payments for the selected holding.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A token on one network is not automatically interchangeable with the same token name on another network.
- If an asset or action is missing, use the options actually offered in your account and contact support if you need clarification.

## Use the FX-rate comparison screen

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

The FX screen helps you compare displayed currency values. Reviewing a rate or changing the list does not itself exchange funds.

1. Open FX Rate and choose the base or comparison currency shown in the amount area.
2. Enter the amount to compare. The mobile amount keyboard includes calculation controls and Done.
3. Choose Add currency, then search or use the Fiat and Crypto sections to add comparison currencies.
4. On mobile, use Pin to top or Delete in the currency-row controls to organize the comparison list.
5. On the web, read the converted amount, displayed rate, and 24-hour change for each row. Check the updating or auto-refresh indicator, and use Refresh now when offered.
6. If the list is empty, use Add currency to populate it. To actually exchange or send money, open that transaction flow and review its own quote.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If your comparison list is empty, add a currency offered by the selector.
- A displayed market comparison may differ from an executable payment quote. Check the quote, fees, and total before authorizing a transaction.

## Find banking, crypto and services from mobile Home

_Applies to: Mobile_

Mobile Home has separate Bank, Crypto and Buburuza views, while the bottom navigation leads to Home, Payments, Chats and Profile.

1. Use Bank, Crypto or Buburuza at the top to choose the overview you need.
2. In Bank, inspect the total balance, then use Send, Receive or Docs. Transactions and All accounts open the corresponding account views.
3. Browse the card carousel to open a card’s Details. An empty card position offers Issue a virtual card or Issue a physical card.
4. In Crypto, check the selected wallet using the wallet dropdown. Use My wallets to switch wallet or begin adding one.
5. Use Favorites, Top Gainers, New or Search to find a token. Manage crypto controls the token/network entries displayed.
6. Use Send, Receive and Swap from Crypto Home, or open a token for its balance and activity.
7. In Buburuza, inspect the displayed BUB balance, Available for Staking and Staked tokens before opening the staking actions.
8. Scroll to Services for FX rate, Concierge, Airport services or eSIM. Pro and referral entries appear separately from these services.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The selected view, wallet and network affect the balances you are reading; an overall value is not the balance of every individual account.
- Zero balance and an unissued card are separate empty states.
- Service and card-issuance tiles are starting points. Check the task-specific guide for the actual setup or purchase flow.

## Compare currencies with the mobile FX calculator

_Applies to: Mobile_

FX Rate compares an entered amount across several currencies. Changing this calculator does not execute a currency exchange.

1. Open FX rate from Services on Home and read Last updated.
2. Check the base currency at the top. Use its selector or move a comparison currency into the base position when you want to change the comparison.
3. Choose Add currency. Search or browse Suggested and the alphabetical list, then select the currencies you want to compare; checkmarks identify selections.
4. Use the pencil beside the base amount to enter a value. The keypad provides numeric and arithmetic controls; choose Done when finished.
5. Review the calculated amount and per-unit rate for each selected currency.
6. Drag a currency by its handle to change its position. Swipe a comparison row aside and choose X to remove it.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Check Last updated before relying on a displayed comparison.
- A zero input produces zero comparison amounts even while per-unit rates remain visible.
- The comparison is separate from a transfer or exchange quote; this page has no transaction submission action.

## Open and name a currency account

_Applies to: Mobile_

Open an account for an offered currency, review its terms, then identify it by a name and icon. Opening an account and adding money are separate tasks.

1. From Bank Home, open All accounts & wallets. Use Search or swipe through the fiat-account cards to find the Open tile.
2. Choose Open and read the Open account introduction. Select Open account to reach the currency list.
3. Search for the currency or choose one from the offered list.
4. Read the currency-specific Terms of use and fee information. Scroll through the terms and open the linked full agreement as needed.
5. If you agree to the presented terms, select the acknowledgement and choose I agree when enabled.
6. Check the new account screen and its balance. A new account may show No transactions yet and Top up wallet.
7. Open Customize to select an icon and change the account name, then Save. Use Close to leave the customization sheet.
8. Use the account’s Top up, Convert or Send action for the next task and review that action separately.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If I agree is unavailable, check the required reading/acknowledgement state before proceeding.
- A newly opened account can have a zero balance and empty history.
- Select a currency currently offered for your account.

## Find, customize and show currency accounts

_Applies to: Mobile_

Use account names, currencies and wallet-network labels to find the intended balance. Visibility controls change the list you see; they are separate from closing an account.

1. Open All accounts & wallets and use Search or the fiat-account carousel to locate the account.
2. For crypto, expand the wallet and compare both asset and network. The same token symbol can appear on more than one network.
3. Open a currency account to see its balance, Top up, Convert, Send and activity.
4. Use Customize to choose an icon or edit the name, then Save.
5. In the Fiat management list, use Search to find a currency and change its visibility toggle as intended.
6. Open Transaction history from the account to review account-filtered activity, then use the available search and filters to narrow the records.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Do not treat hiding an account in the list as confirmation that the account is closed.
- An empty activity list can mean there have been no transactions in the selected account.
- Display currency, account currency and a crypto asset’s network identify different things. Check the destination before using Send or Receive.

## Open and manage a currency account on the web

_Applies to: Web_

All accounts & wallets brings fiat accounts and crypto wallets together. The account-opening route includes a currency choice, terms and a separate creation confirmation.

1. Open All accounts & wallets and choose Open for a new fiat account.
2. Read the new-account introduction and choose Open account. Search and select the intended currency.
3. Read the current Terms of use, including fees and agreements. Review the agreement checkbox before accepting and choosing Create an account.
4. Check the account-opened confirmation and new account tile. The follow-up offers Share or Skip for account access.
5. Open the account for Top up, Convert, Send, activity and Download statement.
6. Use Customize to select an icon and account name, then Save.
7. For a spending limit, choose its period and amount, then Set. Check the success notice, usage counters and Edit/Delete controls.
8. For Multi-sig, enter the amount, select the trusted person, review the summary and Set up. Inspect its enabled state and management controls afterward.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- An account-opening introduction is different from the account-opened confirmation.
- Choose a currency offered by the account’s current selector.
- Read the scope of each account setting before changing it. If a payment-channel control is unclear, ask support how it affects that account.
- Before using Terminate account, check its requirements and how remaining balances or pending payments are handled.
