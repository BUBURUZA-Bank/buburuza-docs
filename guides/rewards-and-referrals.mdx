## View your rewards and completed tasks

_Applies to: Mobile_

The rewards area shows points and tasks in the mobile app. Use the task details and displayed progress to see what is recorded for your account.

1. Open Rewards from Home or Settings.
2. Read Bubu points, Lifetime, and This week as separate totals.
3. Under Complete to earn points, open or review the activity you want to complete and its displayed point amount.
4. After the activity, inspect Completed tasks for its recorded result. Tasks can include funding an account and making a first payment.
5. If no activity has been recorded, the page shows No completed tasks yet. Compare the activity with its underlying transaction before contacting support.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If an activity is not marked complete, check the task requirements and the underlying transaction status.
- If points still appear incorrect, contact support with the task name and relevant transaction reference.

## Share your referral code and track invitation earnings

_Applies to: Mobile_

The referral area separates sharing your code from reviewing invitations and credited earnings. The mobile referral view groups earnings by referral level.

1. Open Referrals and review Total earnings and the Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 summaries.
2. Choose Share to open Your referral code. Use Copy to copy the code or Share to open the sharing route.
3. Use QR code when you want to show the referral QR view; check the displayed profile and use Share if needed.
4. Open a level’s detail page to review Total invited, Total earned and earnings attributed to individual invitees.
5. Open Invites history and choose All Levels, Level 1, Level 2 or Level 3 to filter the activity.
6. Use How referrals work to read the programme structure, and review the currently applicable conditions before expecting a reward.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A shared code or QR image is not proof that an invitation has qualified for a reward.
- Invited counts, per-person earnings and total earnings are different measures. Check the selected level and history filter.
- Use the current programme terms for eligibility, qualification, rates and payout. Do not calculate an entitlement from a promotional example.
