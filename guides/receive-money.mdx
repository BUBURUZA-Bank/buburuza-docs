## Receive money by bank transfer

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Give the sender the receiving instructions shown for the account you want to fund. Check the correct account and payment method before sharing them.

1. Choose Receive or Deposit money and select the destination bank account.
2. In the mobile Add money in layout, select Fiat and the currency, choose Regular bank transfer, then search for the Account to fund.
3. Choose Domestic on mobile or Domestic transfer on the web. The web instructions identify this as the receiving-details screen for domestic wires and ACH.
4. In the mobile Account to fund details, use the individual copy buttons for Routing number, Bank, Account type, Account number and Address. Use Copy or Share for the details together, or Download for the provided document.
5. Check the beneficiary name and address, account number and type, ABA routing number, and receiving-bank name and address.
6. On mobile, use the available copy or Share controls. On the web, use Download as PDF for a document or Share to open Share deposit details.
7. If sharing by email on the web, enter the recipient email address, add an optional message, and choose Send.
8. After the sender initiates payment, check the incoming transaction and available balance for the actual result.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A sender’s confirmation does not by itself show that money is available in Buburuza. Check the incoming transaction and balance.
- If the transfer is missing or pending, contact support with the date, amount, currency, and a masked transfer reference. Use a secure upload only if support requests further documents.

## Receive crypto into a wallet

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Use the receive screen for the exact crypto asset and network you intend to receive. The receive screen provides an address and QR code for sharing with the sender.

1. Open Receive or Deposit money and choose Crypto on-chain, or open a wallet and choose Receive.
2. Choose the destination wallet, asset, and network. On mobile, the flow includes Select destination and Select currency.
3. In the mobile method chooser, select Deposit to display the selected asset’s receiving address and QR code.
4. Read the warning for that exact asset and network. Check the address again after changing either selection.
5. Use Copy or Copy address, or Show QR-code where that control is offered.
6. To include an amount in the mobile request, choose Set amount, enter the token amount and check its fiat equivalent, then choose Set. The requested amount appears as a chip; use X to remove it.
7. On the web, Share opens Share wallet address so you can enter an email address and an optional message; the form describes sharing the address and QR code.
8. Check the incoming transaction after the sender submits it. Network confirmation and posting to the account can be separate states.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the sender used different receiving instructions, contact support with the transaction reference and selected network. Do not assume the transfer can be recovered.
- Never send a wallet recovery phrase or private key to support.
- Setting a requested amount or sharing a QR code does not itself transfer money or prove a deposit has arrived.

## My payment is pending or under review

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Pending can refer to different stages. Read the explanation in the transaction timeline to see whether the transfer is still on a payment network, awaiting confirmation, or posting to your Buburuza account.

1. Open the existing transaction and read its Status timeline.
2. For crypto, distinguish Awaiting confirmation from Awaiting account posting. The latter is shown after on-chain confirmation while the account posting is still pending.
3. For a bank transfer, read the clearing or network-processing message and the arrival estimate specific to that record.
4. Check for an account notice that requests information, verification, acceptance of updated terms, or a limit change.
5. If the state is unclear, contact support with the transaction reference and current message. Check the existing payment before creating another one.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A pending incoming transfer is not the same as available balance.
- An on-chain confirmation count alone does not establish that the transfer has posted to your account.
- An arrival estimate on a payment is not a blanket service guarantee for every payment.

## Top up a mobile currency account with a card or Apple Pay

_Applies to: Mobile_

Choose the destination account and funding method, enter an amount, then continue to the actual payment provider.

1. Open Receive from Bank Home, choose Fiat and the currency you want to add money to.
2. Choose Apple Pay or Debit card from Choose method.
3. For a card, choose a recently used masked card or New card. A new card asks for Card number, Expiration date (MM/YY) and CVV.
4. Choose whether to enable Save this card for future topups, then choose Continue when the form is complete.
5. Enter the amount using the keypad or an offered preset. Check the destination currency and payment method.
6. Choose Continue when enabled and follow the payment-provider instructions shown in the app.
7. Check the actual transaction and balance for the outcome before starting another top-up.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Continue is disabled for zero amounts and incomplete new-card details.
- The saved-card list has X remove controls; check that you are selecting the intended card.
- Reaching a payment-provider screen does not prove a top-up succeeded.

## Choose a desktop crypto receiving or purchase method

_Applies to: Web_

The crypto receiving panel separates an on-chain deposit from a purchase funded by a card or Apple Pay.

1. Open the crypto receiving panel and search for the intended asset.
2. For an on-chain deposit, choose the asset’s On-Chain Deposit route. Confirm the asset/network and address warning, then Copy or Share the address or QR code. Set amount is optional.
3. For a card purchase, select a recently used card or New card. Enter card number, expiry and CVV, choose whether to save it for future topups, then Continue.
4. For card or Apple Pay funding, enter the amount and review its token or fiat unit and quoted equivalent. Zero amounts leave Continue disabled.
5. Continue to the actual provider flow, then check the resulting account transaction for the outcome.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If you select Binance, read the connection and transfer requirements before proceeding.
- PayPal, Revolut Pay, Paysafe card and Skrill are marked Coming soon in the chooser.
- Complete any Apple Pay or payment-provider authorization and then check the transaction in Buburuza. Opening a provider screen does not confirm settlement.
