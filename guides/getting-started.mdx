## Create your Buburuza account

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Start with an email address you can access. Account setup includes confirming your email, setting up account security, and completing the profile and verification steps shown to you. The information requested depends on the type of account you are opening.

1. Choose Sign up. On the web, enter your email and choose Send me a code; on mobile, enter your email and choose Continue.
2. Enter the six-digit email code. Check the address first. On the web, Change email returns to the address step; on mobile, Open email app lets you choose your mail app.
3. Choose Set up passkey. If you want to use an authenticator, choose Set up 2FA instead on mobile or Set up a code instead on the web, then complete its confirmation.
4. Use Copy or Download to save the recovery codes privately, then confirm that you have saved them. The setup screen says these codes will not be shown again.
5. In the business-account journey, enter your first and last name as shown on your government ID, then the legal company name.
6. Choose Continue to verification to continue with the verification partner. If you select Skip for now, return through the verification prompt on Home or Dashboard when ready; the setup screen explains that moving money remains locked.
7. On mobile, create the six-digit app passcode and respond to the Face ID prompt. This app passcode is separate from the email and authenticator codes.
8. After submitting business details, choose Go to dashboard and read the verification status. A review screen is a submitted application, not an approval.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Already have an account? Return to sign-in with your existing email instead of creating another account.
- If you skip a verification step, check the home screen before trying to move money. An account can be created while payment features remain restricted.

## Complete your profile and verification

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Use the verification prompt in your account to see what information is still needed. A profile that has been submitted for review is different from an approved account.

1. Open the verification banner on Home or Dashboard. It may say Verify, Finish verifying, or show that verification is underway.
2. Review your legal name and the account details requested. Enter business details only when you are completing a business-account journey.
3. Follow the verification instructions and provide the documents requested through that secure flow.
4. Submit the information and return to Home or Dashboard to check the displayed status. Respond to any further request in your account.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A review status does not mean the account was rejected. Check for an outstanding information request.
- If information is incorrect and cannot be edited, contact support through Get help. Describe the field that needs correcting; do not send identity documents in the initial support message.

## I have not received my email verification code

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

The email-code screen confirms which address Buburuza is using. Check that address before requesting another code.

1. Keep the code-entry screen open and confirm the displayed email address.
2. Check your inbox and spam or junk folder. If your screen offers Open email, use it to open your mail app.
3. If the email address is wrong, return to the previous step and correct it.
4. Use Resend when that option becomes available, then enter the code from the most recent message into Buburuza.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the code is rejected, check that you copied it correctly and that you are using the latest message. Request a new code if the screen says it has expired.
- If no message arrives after resending, contact support with the approximate time and the error shown. Never include the verification code itself.

## Unlock the mobile app with your passcode or biometrics

_Applies to: Mobile_

The mobile app has an unlock step separate from the initial account sign-in. The screen may offer Face ID or ask for your app passcode.

1. Open the mobile app.
2. Complete the biometric prompt when it is offered and you want to use it.
3. If the app shows a passcode screen, enter your app passcode in the app.
4. Once Home opens, confirm you are in the account you intend to use before viewing details or making a payment.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If biometrics are unavailable, use the fallback displayed by the app.
- If you cannot remember the app passcode, follow the recovery option shown or contact support. Do not keep guessing if the app warns about failed attempts.

## Follow the web app’s introduction

_Applies to: Web_

The web introduction walks through funding an account, saving recipients, and making bank or crypto payments.

1. When the welcome tour appears, read the introductory panel and choose Next.
2. At Fund your account, review the Deposit or receiving-details entry point.
3. At Save your recipients, review how saved bank or wallet details can be reused for payments.
4. At Pay in fiat and crypto, review the available payment entry point.
5. Use Back to revisit a panel or Finish to close the final panel.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The tour explains navigation; it does not itself fund an account, add a recipient, or send a payment.
- If you have finished or closed the tour, use the task guides to find the feature you need.

## Set a company display name and Buburuza callsign

_Applies to: Web_

The business setup described here asks for a company display name and callsign before the later legal-company information.

1. On What is your company’s name?, enter the company display name. The screen explains that legal or trade-name details are collected later.
2. Read the authority acknowledgment before choosing Next.
3. On Set your Buburuza callsign, review the proposed callsign. It is used for the bill-forwarding email address and referral link, and is separate from the name on statements.
4. Answer the company-status and business-activity questions accurately. The list includes public-company status or ownership and selected business categories, with None of the above when accurate.
5. Continue through the setup actually provided and complete the later legal-company information when requested.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The company display name and legal or trade name are different fields.
- Answering the business questions does not by itself confirm eligibility. Check the subsequent verification status.
- This is a business setup route; it does not require a personal-account customer to create a company.
