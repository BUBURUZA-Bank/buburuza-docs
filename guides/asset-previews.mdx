## Understand the available-assets preview

_Applies to: Mobile_

Available assets summarizes an asset’s funding status and preview information. Review that information and the asset’s details before considering an investment.

1. Open Available assets when this view is offered. Use Search to look for an asset; microphone and filter controls also appear beside the search field.
2. Review the asset name and image, Funding open status, remaining time and funding-progress indicator.
3. Read You Invested separately from Total cost. The preview also shows Estimated APY; an estimate is not a guaranteed return.
4. Use Details to look for more information about the selected asset. Review the actual offering information and risks before using any Invest action.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A funding-progress bar or an Invest button does not show that you have completed an investment.
- This preview does not explain ownership, eligibility, fees, withdrawal, losses or how an estimated return is calculated. Do not infer those terms from the summary card.
- If Details does not provide the information needed to understand the offering, stop before an investment and ask the product’s support team for the applicable information.
