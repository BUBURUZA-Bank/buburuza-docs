## Choose, rename and manage a crypto wallet

_Applies to: Mobile_

The wallet selector separates each wallet’s balances, settings and backup status. Token visibility is managed separately from wallet removal.

1. On Crypto Home, open the selected-wallet dropdown to view My wallets.
2. Choose a wallet to inspect its balance, or use the gear beside that wallet to open Settings.
3. In Settings, choose an icon or edit Name. Use Save changes and check Saved.
4. Review Backup to iCloud and Backup manually separately. Each shows its own Not set or Active state.
5. From the crypto token list, choose Manage crypto. Search for the intended asset/network and change its display toggle.
6. To add a wallet, return to My wallets and choose Create new wallet or Add existing wallet. Choose the appropriate wallet method.
7. If removing a wallet, use Delete wallet from that wallet’s Settings. Read the Back up your seed phrase warning and choose Backup to iCloud, Backup manually or Proceed anyway as appropriate.
8. When Enter passcode appears, enter your app passcode. Check the Wallet deleted result and the remaining wallet list after completing removal.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A wallet with No tokens yet can still exist; the empty state offers Manage and Buy.
- Hiding a token does not sell it, transfer it or remove it from the blockchain.
- Before removing a wallet, verify the recovery method for that exact wallet. Wallet removal and deleting an iCloud backup are different operations.
- Swift wallet passkeys and ordinary secret-phrase wallets use different recovery concepts. Do not treat account-login passkeys as a substitute for a wallet backup.
- If Passcode is incorrect appears, check that you are using the app passcode. Follow the current screen’s remaining-attempt guidance; do not guess repeatedly.

## Open a token’s information screen

_Applies to: Mobile_

A token page combines its quoted price, your network-specific balance and recent activity, with shortcuts into crypto actions.

1. Open Crypto Home, check the selected wallet and open the intended token/network entry.
2. Read the token name, quoted price, currency pair, and change indicator.
3. Choose 1D, 1W, 1M, 6M or 1Y to inspect the corresponding chart period.
4. In My balance, check the quantity, fiat equivalent and network.
5. Open Latest Transactions to inspect activity for the token, including signed amounts and categories.
6. Choose Send, Receive, Swap or Buy to begin that separate task. Review the destination flow before confirming a transaction.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The same token symbol can exist on multiple networks; check the network in My balance.
- Price, price movement, balance and transaction amounts describe different things.
- The chart is not proof of an executable quote or a future price. Review the actual transaction quote and fees in the next flow.

## Buy crypto with an account, card or Apple Pay

_Applies to: Mobile_

The mobile Buy flow offers Buburuza funding sources and external payment methods. Review the asset amount, funding source and fees before authorizing the purchase.

1. Open the token page and choose Buy. The Receive → Crypto → Buy route also leads to purchase funding.
2. In Choose method, select an offered Buburuza account/card or external method. Select many is available for choosing multiple internal sources; check the combined balance.
3. For an external card, select a saved masked card or New card. Enter Card number, Expiration date and CVV, choose whether to save it for future topups, and continue when enabled.
4. Enter the amount with the keypad. Use the unit switch to review a fiat-denominated amount and its token equivalent, or a token amount and its fiat equivalent. Check the funding method after changing it.
5. On Review, inspect Buy with, token Amount, Pay fee with, You pay and displayed fees. External routes also show Total.
6. For the internal route, use the Buy slider after checking the quote. For Apple Pay, choose Continue to Apple pay. Other external routes use Continue to reach the actual payment service.
7. Check In Progress or Success for the existing purchase. In Progress says crypto is being sent to your account; use Track or Check details. Success also offers Attach, Receipt, a private Note and Copy link.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Zero amounts leave Continue disabled. Check the entered amount and its unit.
- PayPal, Revolut, Paysafe and Skrill are marked Coming soon in this method chooser. A listed name is not a completed payment option.
- If the funding source or quote differs from what you intended, go back before authorizing.
- If the purchase remains In Progress, inspect that purchase before making another.

## Transfer to staking or withdraw from staking

_Applies to: Mobile_

The Buburuza home view separates the balance available for staking from the amount already staked. Transfer and withdrawal have separate amount-entry and confirmation flows.

1. Open Home and choose Buburuza. Check Available for staking and the Staked tokens section.
2. To add funds, choose Transfer to staking or Add. Check Available balance, enter the token amount or choose 25%, 50%, 75% or Max, and inspect the fiat equivalent.
3. Use the Transfer slider once the amount is entered and correct. Check Transferred to staking, its timestamp and amount, then choose Close.
4. To remove funds from staking, choose Withdraw. Check the displayed withdrawable amount, enter the amount or choose a percentage/Max, and inspect the equivalent.
5. Use the Withdraw slider when the amount is correct. Check Withdrawn from staking, its timestamp and amount, then choose Close.
6. Return to the Buburuza overview to inspect the available and staked balances.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The Transfer and Withdraw sliders are inactive in the shown empty-amount states.
- Available for staking and withdrawable amounts are different fields; check the one that applies to your action.
- Read the applicable staking terms for rewards, fees and availability. The amount controls and success records do not establish a guaranteed yield or an unbonding policy.

## Review and confirm a crypto swap

_Applies to: Mobile_

The swap flow lets you choose source and destination assets and networks, enter an amount, and review the quote and fees before confirmation.

1. Open Swap and check the source asset and network and the destination asset and network.
2. Enter the source amount or choose 25%, 50%, or Max. Check the direction; use the reverse-direction control if you intend to swap the opposite way.
3. Review the expected destination amount, displayed exchange rate, and Network fee, then choose Next.
4. On review, check both amounts, both assets and networks, Network fee, Provider fee, and Minimum Received.
5. Open View details when you need the additional fee information shown by the interface.
6. Choose Confirm Swap only when the reviewed terms match your intended swap.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Minimum Received is a separate value from the expected quote; review both before confirming.
- Use Back or close to leave the amount-entry or review screen before confirming. After confirmation, inspect the transaction’s status before taking further action.
- If you see a pending, failed, expired-quote or insufficient-balance message, follow the explanation shown for that swap. Check the existing transaction before retrying, and ask support if its outcome or a slippage setting is unclear.
