Choose the task you need help with. Check the selected space and account before changing settings or moving money.

- [Set up your account](/onboarding-and-security) — Sign up, enroll a credential and complete the verification requested for your space.
- [Sign in and protect your account](/guides/account-access) — Use passwordless sign-in, manage passkeys and review sessions.
- [Accounts and wallets](/accounts-wallets) — Read your available, pending and held balances and find account documents.
- [Move money](/payments) — Review funding instructions, payment details, fees and the existing transaction status before retrying.
- [Teams and spaces](/team) — Manage invitations and understand what each role can do.
- [Settings and support](/guides/settings-and-support) — Find the settings available to your account and send a support request.

Cards, lending, interest, yield and investment products are not offered. See [Legal and regulatory](/legal) for the product boundary and market availability.
