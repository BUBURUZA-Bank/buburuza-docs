Start with the task you want to complete. Each guide identifies mobile and web instructions where they differ.

- [Start using Buburuza](/guides/getting-started) — Set up access, complete the information requested for your account, and check the home-screen status before moving money. Business verification and personal verification are separate journeys; complete the one presented for the account you are opening.
- [Sign in and protect your account](/guides/account-access) — Choose an available sign-in method, keep a usable recovery route, and review sessions when you change devices. The mobile unlock passcode is separate from account recovery.
- [Find your accounts, wallets, and balances](/guides/accounts-and-balances) — Start by confirming the selected profile. Then choose the specific bank account or crypto wallet. Read the asset, network, currency and balance labels together before choosing an action.
- [Receive money and confirm arrival](/guides/receive-money) — Choose the receiving destination first. Share the instructions for that destination and payment method, then follow incoming activity until the transaction and available balance show the outcome.
- [Prepare and maintain recipients](/guides/recipients) — Store the destination details for the person or business you intend to pay. Confirm changes through a trusted channel and review the selected payment method again on each payment.
- [Send a bank payment](/guides/send-bank-payments) — Choose a recipient and a payment method, complete the required fields, and inspect the review before authorizing the transfer. The appropriate bank details, fee presentation and currency fields depend on the payment method.
- [Send crypto from a wallet](/guides/send-crypto) — Start with the exact asset and network, then confirm the receiving address and payment total. Use the transaction record to follow the outcome after authorization.
- [Turn an invoice into a reviewed payment](/guides/invoice-payments) — Invoice reading helps prepare the payment. It does not replace checking the recipient and amount or authorizing the transaction. Follow the same final review used for a manually entered payment.
- [Use mobile payment tools](/guides/mobile-payment-tools) — Mobile payment tools include transfers to users, transfers between accounts, scheduled payments, requests, links and scanning. Treat the outcome of each tool separately: a request, a schedule or a shared link is not a completed transfer.
- [Understand transactions and solve payment problems](/guides/transactions) — Find the existing record before retrying a payment. Check its account, method and status, then collect the reference and error details if support needs to investigate.
- [Review cards, limits, and approvals](/guides/cards-and-controls) — Card controls, account limits and trusted-person approvals have different scopes. Check which control is affecting the payment and review the resulting state after any change.
- [Find statements and account documents](/guides/documents) — Use the document category and period to locate the right record. Keep identity and tax information within the secure process presented for your account.
- [Manage crypto and explore available services](/guides/crypto-features) — Manage wallets, purchases, token information, staking and flexible loans. Read the terms, eligibility and risk information for the service you intend to use before authorizing a transaction.
- [Choose a data plan and install an eSIM](/guides/esim) — Choose a destination plan, review its payment, and then check the eSIM installation state. Purchasing data and installing the eSIM are separate steps.
- [Find and book airport services](/guides/airport-services) — Check the airport, terminal and access conditions for lounges. For a fast-track booking, review the date, time, passenger details, price or pass entitlement, and delivery options before confirming.
- [Use chats, concierge, and B1](/guides/chats-and-assistance) — Use the appropriate conversation for the task, then review a separate payment request or authorization when money is involved. Check actual account activity before relying on a conversational description of a transaction.
- [Track rewards and invitations](/guides/rewards-and-referrals) — Read the offer requirements before expecting points or referral rewards. Keep invitation status, task completion, points credited and any redemption separate.
- [Manage settings and get help](/guides/settings-and-support) — Review editable profile fields, notification preferences and appearance. Use the official help route for locked fields, account closure questions and issues that need investigation.
- [Back up and recover a crypto wallet](/guides/wallet-backup-and-recovery) — Identify the wallet type and recovery method, create or verify a backup, and keep wallet recovery separate from account sign-in.
- [Prepare and manage a crypto loan](/guides/crypto-loans) — Review collateral, interest and loan-to-value information, then follow loan approval, adjustment and repayment as separate tasks.
- [Complete personal setup on mobile](/guides/personal-account-setup) — Register contact details, read the verification status, and finish address and nickname setup in the personal mobile flow.
- [Understand the asset preview](/guides/asset-previews) — Browse asset summaries and use Details to learn more. Review the offering information before considering an investment; the preview is not an investment confirmation.

## Need help?

If you need help with your account or a task, [contact support](/support) or browse the [Buburuza Help Center](https://help.buburuza.com/hc/en-us).
