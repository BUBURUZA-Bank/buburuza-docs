## Loan amount, collateral and repayment are separate decisions

A flexible loan has an outstanding amount and a set of pledged collateral assets. The loan screen presents APR, estimated daily interest, and loan-to-value levels. Adding collateral changes the assets supporting the loan; a repayment reduces the debt.

A repayment can use the loan crypto or collateral. Check **Loan after repaying** before confirming. Using **Max** can spend the available repayment balance while leaving a residual loan. **Repayment successful** therefore differs from a loan being repaid in full and moved to **Settled loans**.

The warning and high-risk screens show risk before liquidation. Read the current LTV, margin-call and liquidation levels for the loan and review its service terms. The displayed thresholds, repayment preview and collateral allocation belong to that specific review; they are not universal product values.

## Prepare and review a flexible crypto loan

_Applies to: Mobile_

The Flexible loan flow asks you to choose a loan amount and asset, provide collateral, and review interest and loan-to-value information before confirming.

1. Open Flexible loan. Use Borrow now from the empty loan list, or open the borrowing form available in the loan area.
2. Choose the loan asset. The selector separates Stablecoins and Crypto; use the assets offered to you.
3. Enter Loan amount. Review Remaining limit, Latest APR and Estimated daily interest.
4. If the amount is below the displayed minimum, correct it. Once the amount is accepted, choose Add collateral.
5. Select the assets and amounts you intend to pledge. Check the collateral total and any shortfall before choosing Add.
6. Back on the loan form, inspect Collateral amount, Pledgeable balance, Current LTV, Margin call LTV and Liquidation LTV. Use the LTV information control when needed.
7. Choose Review loan. Recheck the amount, asset, APR, daily-interest estimate, collateral and all LTV levels.
8. Open Crypto Loan Service Terms and Forced repayment rules and read them. Complete the acknowledgement only if you accept the terms, then use the Confirm slider to submit the loan.
9. On Loan approved, verify the amount and destination. Choose Go to wallet or Back to Flexible loan and inspect the loan record.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Entering a valid loan amount does not complete the loan. Review loan remains disabled until sufficient collateral has been provided.
- Check the minimum, remaining limit, APR and thresholds shown for your loan.
- The approval result and the wallet’s updated transaction details can appear at different times. Inspect the existing record before starting a duplicate request.
- Borrow more opens another borrowing form; it is not a repayment control.

## Choose or adjust collateral for a loan

_Applies to: Mobile_

Collateral selection lets you pledge more than one asset and choose how much of each asset to include. The form previews whether the combined value is sufficient.

1. For a new loan, choose Add collateral. For an existing loan, open Loan details, choose Collateral, then Adjust.
2. Use Search or browse the current wallet’s asset/network entries. Check the intended assets, or use Select all only when you intend to select the full available group.
3. To change one asset’s amount, open its adjustment control. Check Available balance, enter an amount or use 25%, 50%, 75% or Max.
4. Use the unit-switch control if you want to enter the amount in fiat value rather than crypto units. Review both representations, then choose Set.
5. Check the combined Collateral amount against the loan amount and the displayed shortfall. Add or increase collateral if the total is insufficient.
6. For a new loan, choose Add when enabled and review the resulting LTV on the loan form.
7. For an existing loan, choose Save when your changes are ready. Use Discard changes to leave without applying the adjustment.
8. Return to Collateral and verify the asset list, total collateral and updated Current LTV.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Checking an asset and choosing an amount are separate decisions; a selected partial amount can still leave a shortfall.
- Add or Save is disabled in the shown insufficient-collateral states.
- The same asset on different networks is shown as a separate entry; check the intended asset/network.
- An updated collateral preview does not guarantee its future value. Monitor the loan’s current metrics after saving.

## Repay a loan with the borrowed crypto asset

_Applies to: Mobile_

With loan crypto uses the loan asset for repayment. Review the remaining loan amount; using Max may use your available balance without paying off the whole loan.

1. Open the active loan, check its asset and outstanding amount, and choose Repay.
2. Select With loan crypto.
3. Enter the repayment amount or choose Max. Check Available balance and the repayment amount.
4. Review Current LTV, Updated LTV and Updated loan, then choose Continue.
5. On Review, check You’re repaying, Loan after repaying, Updated LTV, Liquidation LTV and Liquidation price.
6. Use the Repay loan slider when the review matches your intention.
7. On Repayment successful, choose Check details or Back to Flexible loan. Inspect the remaining loan amount.
8. If a balance remains, the loan stays active and still offers Repay. Treat it as settled only after the record shows it has been repaid in full and closed.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Max uses the amount available for repayment. Check Loan after repaying because debt can remain outstanding.
- A repayment-success message can describe a partial repayment.
- The displayed Updated loan value is the clearest preview of the residual loan before you confirm.

## Repay a loan using collateral

_Applies to: Mobile_

With collateral is a separate repayment route from using the borrowed crypto asset. Check the selected collateral and resulting loan before confirming.

1. Open the active loan and choose Repay.
2. Select With collateral.
3. Review the collateral value and select the collateral asset shown for the repayment.
4. Enter the repayment amount or use the available amount control. Check Updated loan and Updated LTV.
5. Choose Continue when the amount is complete.
6. On Review, check You’re repaying, Loan after repaying, Updated LTV, Liquidation LTV and Liquidation price.
7. Use the Repay loan slider when the reviewed repayment matches your intention.
8. Check Repayment successful and the resulting loan record. If fully repaid, verify the closed state or Settled loans entry labelled Repaid with collateral.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Repaying with collateral is different from simply adding collateral to reduce LTV.
- Read the loan terms for conversion, sale, slippage and fee consequences before confirming this route.
- A repayment amount that leaves an Updated loan balance is a partial repayment even if the transaction succeeds.

## Check an active, partly repaid or settled loan

_Applies to: Mobile_

Active loans and Settled loans distinguish current debt from a loan that has been repaid in full. A successful payment can leave a loan active if a balance remains.

1. Open Flexible loan and choose Active loans or Settled loans.
2. In Active loans, check the asset, outstanding amount and current-LTV state, then open the loan.
3. Use Loan details to review the loan amount, average/latest APR, estimated daily interest, collateral value and LTV metrics.
4. Switch to Collateral to inspect pledged assets and their total value.
5. After a repayment, check the remaining loan amount. A residual amount with a Repay action means the loan is still active.
6. After full repayment, look for the repaid-and-closed result and the entry in Settled loans. A collateral repayment entry identifies Repaid with collateral and its timestamp.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- No active loans and No settled loans yet are separate empty states.
- An outstanding amount can remain after choosing Max when the available repayment balance is smaller than the debt.
- Loan approved, Repayment successful and Loan closed describe different stages.

## Understand and review a loan LTV warning

_Applies to: Mobile_

Loan details shows Current LTV alongside Margin call LTV and Liquidation LTV. Read the displayed warning and high-risk states to understand how close the loan is to its thresholds.

1. Open the Warning or High risk loan row from Crypto Home, or open the active loan directly.
2. Read the warning banner and compare Current LTV with the margin-call and liquidation levels displayed for that loan.
3. Open the LTV information control to read the explanation of borrowing relative to collateral value.
4. To inspect collateral changes, open Collateral and choose Adjust. Review available assets and amounts, the shortfall and the resulting collateral total.
5. When you intend to apply a sufficient adjustment, choose Save and inspect the updated Current LTV on the loan.
6. If considering repayment instead, use the separate Repay flow and review its Updated loan and Updated LTV before confirming.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The amber warning and red high-risk state are different from a completed liquidation statement.
- An insufficient adjustment leaves Save disabled; selecting an asset is not enough unless the displayed total qualifies.
- Read the loan terms for margin calls, liquidation and forced repayment. Check your loan’s thresholds and do not assume a warning guarantees time to act.
