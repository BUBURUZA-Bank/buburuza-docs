## Backup state and wallet removal

Each wallet shows **Backup to iCloud** and **Backup manually** independently. One may be Active while the other is Not set. The status belongs to the selected wallet; switching wallets can show a different backup configuration.

For an iCloud backup, check its timestamp and use **Verify** to test the password. For a manual backup, the confirmation asks you to identify words at selected positions. A wrong selection has a **Try again** path.

Removing a wallet from the app and deleting an iCloud backup are different actions. The wallet-removal flow asks you to back up the seed phrase before proceeding. The iCloud-backup deletion warning explains that the deleted backup can no longer be used to restore the wallet. Verify the recovery method you intend to keep before removing either item.

## Create and verify an iCloud wallet backup

_Applies to: Mobile_

The iCloud backup protects a wallet’s secret phrase with a separate password. Buburuza cannot reset that backup password.

1. Open the intended wallet’s Settings and choose Backup to iCloud.
2. Complete the app authentication if requested. Enter a recognizable Backup name, then choose Continue.
3. On Set password, create the password that will encrypt the secret phrase on iCloud. Use the visibility control only in a private setting, then choose Continue.
4. On Confirm password, repeat the password and read both acknowledgements about recovery and loss of access. Check them only after you understand them, then choose Continue.
5. Return to wallet Settings and check that Backup to iCloud is Active.
6. Open the active backup to inspect its timestamp and choose Verify.
7. Enter the backup password on Verify backup password and choose Continue. Check Password verified, then choose Done.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The backup-encryption password is different from your app passcode and card PIN.
- Continue is disabled in the shown empty password and incomplete confirmation states.
- An Active iCloud status and an Active manual backup are independent; checking one does not complete the other.
- Do not send the phrase, encryption password or a screenshot of either to support.

## Write down and verify a wallet’s secret phrase

_Applies to: Mobile_

A manual backup records the wallet’s secret phrase. Anyone who obtains that phrase can control the wallet, according to the safety screens.

1. Open the intended wallet’s Settings and choose Backup manually.
2. Complete app authentication when requested. Read the For your eyes only safety advice.
3. Read and complete the acknowledgements: Buburuza does not have access to the key, and the phrase should be written on paper and stored securely instead of saved digitally. Choose Continue.
4. Record the numbered secret words in their original order in a private place. Do not share them or include them in support messages.
5. Choose Continue to open Confirm secret phrase. For each requested position, choose the matching word from your written backup.
6. Choose Continue. If Not correct / Your selections don’t match appears, choose Try again and compare the requested positions with the backup.
7. Return to Settings and check the manual-backup status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The confirmation asks for selected word positions; it does not ask you to send the full phrase to support.
- If the word check reports an incorrect answer, choose Try again and verify the requested word positions against your backup.
- Record every word shown for your own wallet in the original order. Confirm the exact positions requested by that wallet’s backup check.
- An incorrect app passcode has a separate remaining-attempt warning. Do not confuse it with the phrase-confirmation quiz.

## Import an existing wallet with its secret phrase

_Applies to: Mobile_

Add existing wallet offers several import methods. For a secret-phrase import, select the wallet network, name the wallet and enter its existing phrase.

1. Open My wallets and choose Add existing wallet, then Secret phrase.
2. Read the safety acknowledgements: only you should know the phrase, it must not have been supplied by a representative, and someone who sees it can steal funds. Choose Continue after acknowledging them.
3. On Select network, search for the required network or choose the recommended Multi-coin wallet option when it matches your wallet.
4. Choose an icon and enter Wallet name. Enter or paste the Secret phrase into the app’s field, preserving word order and spaces.
5. If using the scan option, align the QR code in Scan QR-code. The screen also offers a photo-library icon and Enter code manually.
6. For manual code entry, complete the field and choose Confirm. Empty Confirm is disabled.
7. Review the intended wallet/network and choose Continue when the fields are complete.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Only enter your own phrase in the official wallet-import flow. Never send it to support or enter a phrase supplied by someone else.
- The helper describes phrases as usually twelve and sometimes eighteen or twenty-four words; match your original wallet backup.

## Understand a Swift wallet passkey

_Applies to: Mobile_

The Swift wallet setup treats a passkey as the key to that wallet. It is separate from a passkey used only to sign in to your Buburuza account.

1. Choose Swift from the wallet creation or existing-wallet choices shown in My wallets.
2. Read Passkey is the key to your wallet.
3. Review the three acknowledgements: enable iCloud Passwords and Keychain, use the passkey for recovery on a replacement device, and understand the warning about losing wallet access if the passkey is deleted.
4. Complete the Quick quiz about deleting a wallet passkey. If Wrong answer appears, choose Try again; if Correct appears, choose Got it.
5. On Set wallet, choose an icon and Wallet name. The shown name rule is four to twenty-four characters. Choose Continue.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Deleting a Swift wallet passkey can cause loss of access to the wallet and funds. Keep wallet recovery separate from account-login passkey management.
- Keep ordinary account authentication, app unlocking and wallet recovery separate.
- The quiz checks understanding. Passing it does not establish that a device backup or restoration has been tested.

## Delete an iCloud wallet backup

_Applies to: Mobile_

Deleting an iCloud backup removes that recovery copy. It is a different action from removing the wallet from the app.

1. Open the intended wallet’s Settings and its active Backup to iCloud entry.
2. Check the backup timestamp and choose Delete.
3. Read Are you sure? The warning says you cannot restore from this backup after it is removed.
4. Choose Cancel if you need to keep the recovery copy. Choose Delete iCloud backup only when you intend to remove it and understand the warning.
5. Return to Settings and inspect the backup status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Do not assume a manual backup exists merely because iCloud backup is active. Check each backup independently.
- The dialog says a new backup can be created; that does not make a lost or deleted copy recoverable.
- Deleting a backup does not establish that blockchain assets were transferred or destroyed.

## Create a Swift wallet in the desktop wallet panel

_Applies to: Web_

The desktop wallet panel offers a Swift route with safety acknowledgments, a passkey-loss check and wallet naming before the device passkey step.

1. Open My wallets and choose Create new wallet, then Swift.
2. Read the safety acknowledgments about iCloud Passwords and Keychain, replacement-device access and the consequences of deleting the wallet passkey.
3. Answer What happens if passkey is deleted? If Wrong answer appears, choose Try again and reread the warning. On Correct!, choose Got it.
4. In Set wallet, choose an icon and enter a wallet name. This layout asks for between 4 and 24 characters.
5. Choose Continue and follow the actual macOS passkey flow supplied by the product.
6. Check the new zero-balance wallet in My wallets. Hello, new wallet offers Fund my wallet or Skip.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- This passkey controls wallet access; it is different from a credential used only to sign in to the Buburuza account.
- Follow the passkey prompts for your operating system and check that your intended recovery access is in place.
- Fund my wallet is a following entry, not proof that the new wallet already contains funds.

## Import a wallet with a secret phrase on desktop

_Applies to: Web_

The web import route requires a safety check, network choice and the existing wallet’s secret phrase.

1. Open My wallets and choose Add existing wallet, then Secret phrase.
2. Read and confirm the safety statements only if they are accurate: the phrase is yours, it was not supplied by another person or company representative, and you understand the access it grants.
3. Choose the applicable multi-coin option or asset/network in the network selector.
4. Choose an icon/name and enter the existing secret phrase in the dedicated field. The helper describes words separated by spaces; use the phrase’s original word order.
5. Choose Continue and check the imported wallet in My wallets.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Never send a secret phrase to support or paste one provided by another person.
- Use the guide for the import method you select. Private key, iCloud, View-only wallet, Keystore and Swift require their own information; do not substitute a secret phrase into another method’s field.
- A selected network and a pasted phrase are preparation states; check the wallet-list result before assuming the import completed.
