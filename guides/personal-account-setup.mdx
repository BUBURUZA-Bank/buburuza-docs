## Start personal account setup on mobile

_Applies to: Mobile_

The personal mobile setup collects contact details and identity information, then separates account access from identity verification and any remaining onboarding.

1. On welcome, choose Sign up. Select email or the mobile registration option; Google and Apple are separate provider choices when offered.
2. For email, enter your address and choose Continue. Enter the six-digit code sent to the masked address shown by the app.
3. Enter your phone number with its country calling code, then complete Verify your phone number using the code sent there.
4. If Create login password is presented, enter and repeat the password. This flow states at least eight characters. Choose Continue.
5. Select Country of residence and enter the legal first/middle names, legal last names and Day/Month/Year of birth requested under Identity.
6. Read the actual service agreement offered. Complete I have read it and I accept it only when appropriate, then use Swipe to sign if you intend to accept.
7. Create and repeat the six-digit app passcode. Review the optional Enable Face ID and Enable notifications choices, or use Skip for now.
8. Read the outcome. Let’s go! continues accepted access; the waitlist result offers Turn on notifications and does not mean all services are available.
9. Continue identity verification and remaining onboarding from the app’s status prompt.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Login password, six-digit app passcode, card PIN and wallet-backup password are separate credentials.
- Check the masked email or phone destination before resending a code. Use the displayed resend countdown.
- The calling-code list does not establish country eligibility, and the identity fields do not establish approved age or document rules.
- For Google or Apple registration, complete the provider’s authentication prompts and return to Buburuza to check your setup status.

## Understand verification and waitlist status on mobile

_Applies to: Mobile_

The app distinguishes waitlist enrollment, identity-verification progress and completion of the remaining account setup.

1. Check the status shown on Home, Profile or Account.
2. If Verification required appears, choose Verify or Continue to open identity verification.
3. If Verification pending appears, the review has not reached a final result. Check the existing status rather than starting repeated submissions.
4. If Verification has failed appears, open the failure detail and use Try again to restart the verification path. Read any relevant message in the Notifications conversation; Close dismisses the prompt without approving the account.
5. If Verification success! appears, choose Continue. The app can still require address confirmation, nickname and other onboarding steps.
6. If the result says you joined the waitlist, use Turn on notifications if desired and wait for the acceptance notice. Waitlist enrollment is a separate state from accepted service access.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The above status descriptions apply to the current account; check that you are viewing the intended profile.
- A service tile can be visible while its introduction still requires Verify identity.
- Use the status and any next action shown for your verification or waitlist entry. Contact support if you need an update.
- Identity verification success does not, by itself, finish every onboarding step.

## Confirm your address and nickname after verification

_Applies to: Mobile_

After identity verification succeeds, the mobile flow continues with address confirmation and a nickname.

1. Choose Continue from Verification success!.
2. On Your address, use Search or the available location control to choose the address you intend to use for billing, mailing and delivery.
3. On Double check your address, review Country of residence, State, City, Address line 1 and Address line 2. Correct the fields and choose Continue.
4. On Create your nickname, enter the @-prefixed nickname that friends will use to find you, then choose Continue.
5. Review the optional 2FA setup if offered.
6. When the first-home Top up overlay appears, choose Top up to start funding or Close to view Home. The overlay itself does not add funds.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Address line 2 is a separate field; do not substitute a postal code unless the app specifically asks for it.
