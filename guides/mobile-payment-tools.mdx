## Send money to another Buburuza user

_Applies to: Mobile_

Find the intended Buburuza contact, choose the funding source and sending currency, and review the payment before using the Send control.

1. Open Payments. Under Transfer to user, use Search name or phone or select the intended suggested contact.
2. Check the recipient identity, then open Sending from to choose an offered fiat account or asset/network. Use Select many for the multiple-account route.
3. Open Sending currency. Choose Fiat or Crypto and search for the currency or asset you intend to send.
4. Enter the amount or use Max. Compare the available source balance or combined balance and the amount shown.
5. Choose Continue when enabled. On Review, inspect From, To, Amount and the displayed Arrives in information.
6. Use the Send slider after confirming the recipient and payment.
7. Check the result. Use Check details, Receipt or Track for the record; the result also offers Attach, Copy link, a chat entry and a private Internal note.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If contacts look similar, verify the intended person before sending.
- Insufficient funds is shown in red and Continue is disabled when the amount exceeds the selected balance. Reduce the amount or choose sufficient funding.
- Changing the source can change the available balance and conversion; review the sending currency and amount again.
- If the result is unclear, inspect the existing transaction before attempting another transfer.

## Move money between your accounts or wallets

_Applies to: Mobile_

Move or convert between your available fiat accounts and crypto wallets. The flow supports the offered fiat-to-fiat, fiat-to-crypto, crypto-to-fiat and crypto-to-crypto combinations.

1. Open Payments and choose Between accounts. Alternatively, Convert on a currency account preselects the destination; Swap on a token page preselects the source.
2. In Sending from, search for a fiat account or expand the intended crypto wallet and choose its asset/network.
3. In Sending to, select the destination account or wallet asset/network. Check both sides and the direction before entering the amount.
4. Enter an amount or choose Max. Review the available balance, quoted rate and equivalent amount.
5. Use the unit-conversion control to enter the amount in source or destination units. Check the recalculated amount on the other side.
6. Choose Continue. Review From, To, You send, Exchange rate, You receive, Fee and Arrives in.
7. Use the Send slider after checking the review. On Success, check the received amount and destination; use Receipt, Track, Check details, Copy link or the private Internal note as needed.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Zero amounts disable Continue. Insufficient funds appears when the required source amount exceeds the balance; reduce the amount or choose a sufficient source.
- Entering a target amount in destination units can change how much is deducted from the source.
- Use only destinations and asset/network combinations offered by the selector.
- Changing display currency in Appearance does not make this conversion.

## Set up a recurring payment

_Applies to: Mobile_

A recurring payment combines payment instructions with a frequency, start/end settings and an optional reminder. Enabling it can execute a payment immediately when the start date is today.

1. Open Recurring payments and use the add action to start Set up recurring payment.
2. Choose the recipient or payment route from the offered user, bank, crypto or between-account entries. Complete that payment’s instructions.
3. Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly.
4. Check Starts and Ends. Starts is shown as Today and Ends can be No end date. Use the calendar controls to set the intended date values and continue.
5. Set Payment reminders if you want the offered notification before each payment processes.
6. On Review recurring payment, check Type, Starting from, Until, Duration and the transfer information.
7. If Starting from is today, read the warning that a transaction is made after enabling. Use the Enable payment slider only when the schedule and immediate effect match your intention.
8. Check Recurring payment is set, the amount and the frequency/date label. Add an attachment or private Internal note if needed, then choose Close.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Creating a schedule is different from each scheduled transfer completing. Check the individual execution history.
- Check the frequency and start date before enabling; an immediate first payment can be intended by the schedule.
- Reminders are a preference, not proof that a payment processed.
- If Until and Duration disagree, review and correct the schedule before enabling it.

## Request a payment from a person or business

_Applies to: Mobile_

A payment request identifies who should pay, what the payment is for, where it should be deposited and the requested amount. The delivery route depends on the recipient.

1. Open Payments, then Other and Request payment.
2. Search name or phone to choose a Buburuza contact, or choose a saved person/business from Recipients. Use Create if you need to add a recipient.
3. In What is this payment for?, enter a clear description. Add Purpose code only when appropriate, then choose Continue.
4. In Deposit to, choose Fiat or Crypto and select the intended receiving account or token/network. Use Search in the crypto list.
5. Enter the requested amount or select an offered amount preset. Check the displayed minimum and Continue availability. For crypto, check the token amount and fiat equivalent; the unit-switch control changes which unit you enter.
6. On Review, verify Request from, contact details, recipient address where shown, Deposit to and Requested amount.
7. For the personal email route, choose Send link by email. For the business route, choose Send invoice by email. For a Buburuza contact, use Send link to chat.
8. Check Payment link sent!, Invoice sent! or the Payment request card in the chat. Use Download invoice when needed, then Got it or Back to Payments.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Continue stays disabled while the required purpose or amount is empty.
- Email is optional during recipient creation, but email delivery requires a usable address. Check the recipient’s contact information before choosing the email route.
- A sent link, invoice or chat request is not proof that funds arrived. Inspect the receiving transaction separately.
- A crypto equivalent can change with the displayed conversion; no rate-lock promise is established by the amount entry.

## Create and share a payment link

_Applies to: Mobile_

Create a receiving link for the destination account you choose, with an optional amount and expiry, then share its link or QR code.

1. Open Payments and choose Share to open Share payment link.
2. Enter or check the Title. Open Deposit to, search for the fiat account or expand the intended crypto wallet and choose its asset/network.
3. If you want to request a fixed amount, enable Add amount and enter the amount in the unit shown. Otherwise leave the amount option off.
4. If you want an expiry, enable Add expiry date and choose from the options currently offered. This layout offers 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days.
5. Check the title, destination, optional amount and expiry together, then choose Create.
6. On Link ready to share, review Amount to pay and Link validity time. Use the QR code, Send via email, Share or Copy link as needed.
7. Copied to clipboard confirms that the link was copied. Check the receiving transaction and balance separately to confirm actual payment.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Check Title and Deposit to independently so the description matches the intended receiving account.
- If a link no longer works, check its expiry before creating a replacement with current payment information.
- Creating, copying or sharing a link does not establish that the payer received it or paid.
- Use the address and QR generated for the receiving account you intend to use.

## Scan a document or code to prepare a payment

_Applies to: Mobile_

Scan or upload a payment document, review the suggested payment route, and continue through that route’s normal review and authorization.

1. Open Payments and choose Scan to pay.
2. Choose Scan using camera, Upload from photo library or Choose from files.
3. For the camera route, position the document inside the guides and use the shutter. The gallery icon provides access to an existing image.
4. Wait while the document is recognized. The interface highlights detected fields and shows Searching items… with a Cancel option.
5. After Document is scanned, open the attached document preview if needed and inspect Choose a payment rail. The recommendation is labelled Most applicable to your contract; alternatives shown are ACH, Wire, Check and Inter-wire.
6. Choose the applicable payment rail after checking the original document. If a recurring payment is detected, choose Set up a recurring payment to configure it or Skip.
7. Continue through the selected payment flow and verify its recipient, bank details, amount, fees and timing before authorizing.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If Failed to scan. Try again appears, retry or choose another document source.
- A successful scan means the document was recognized, not that money was sent.
- Check extracted information against the original document before completing a payment. A recommendation is not a guarantee that the details are correct.
- Scanning may offer Check as a route, but use it only when the actual cheque-payment instructions are available.

## Choose and order multiple funding accounts

_Applies to: Mobile_

Mobile crypto, ACH, Wire and International wire transfers can use multiple funding accounts. Select the accounts, arrange their priority and review the final deduction separately.

1. In a supported mobile crypto, ACH, Wire or International wire flow, open Sending from and choose Select many.
2. Use Search and the Fiat or expandable Crypto wallet lists to find the intended sources. Check each source you want to use.
3. Review the numbered selections, then choose Done. Continue with multiple accounts is disabled until a source is selected.
4. Drag selected accounts by their handles to change their priority. To remove one, swipe the row aside and choose X.
5. Choose Continue with multiple accounts. Check the source list, sending currency or outgoing asset/network, amount and destination for that transfer.
6. On Deduction method, choose In order or Split evenly and inspect the before/after balances and amount shown.
7. Choose Continue and review the selected sources, destination, amount and fee details before using the Send slider.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Check account order separately from which accounts are selected.
- For In order or Split evenly, inspect the displayed allocation before continuing. If the amounts or conversion are unclear, ask support before authorizing the transfer.
- Changing selection or priority does not itself send funds. The review and Send action are separate.
- Card connected-account order and payment funding order are separate settings. Review the order in the payment itself.
- If selection reaches a displayed plan limit, review the Get Buburuza Pro notice or continue with the permitted number of accounts.

## Pay a request from a payment link

_Applies to: Payer web page_

A payment-link page shows the payee, request title, fixed amount and currency, and validity countdown. Review these details before choosing a payment method.

1. Open the payment link and verify the payee, request title, fixed amount, currency, and validity information.
2. Choose an available payment method. The steps below cover Credit/debit card, Bank transfer and Crypto.
3. For a card payment, enter Card number, Cardholder name, Expiry date, and CVV. Complete Billing details: Address, City, ZIP code, and Country. Recheck the amount, then choose Pay.
4. For a bank transfer, copy the required Account number, Account holder name, Routing number, Bank, and SWIFT code as applicable to the rail. Use Download PDF if you need a copy of the instructions, then initiate the transfer through your bank.
5. For crypto, choose the exact coin and chain, review the displayed rate, and use the address or QR code. The page instructs sending precisely the shown amount in one transaction.
6. After sending crypto, the page offers I've made the transfer. Treat that as a report that you sent it, not proof that it has been received.
7. Use the back arrow to change method before making a payment. Keep the actual payment reference so the sender and recipient can reconcile it.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Copying bank instructions or downloading a PDF does not send money.
- Choosing I've made the transfer does not establish on-chain receipt or settlement.
- If the chooser offers Apple Pay, Revolut Pay, PayPal, Paysafe Card or Skrill, follow the instructions provided after selecting that method. If the next step is unavailable or unclear, stop and contact support before paying.
- If you cannot establish whether payment succeeded, check the payment method’s existing transaction before paying again.

## Inspect a recurring payment and its history

_Applies to: Mobile_

The recurring-payment list separates active frequencies from Deactivated schedules. A schedule detail shows both its configuration and its individual payments.

1. Open Recurring payments and use Search to find the recipient or merchant.
2. Check whether the row is Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly or Deactivated, then open it.
3. Review the amount, frequency, Total paid, End date and Payment method.
4. Open Info to inspect Type, Starting from, Until, Duration and the transfer-specific account fields.
5. Review the payments grouped by date to check an individual execution. Attach and the private Note relate to the schedule record.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A schedule total is different from the status of its latest execution.
- A merchant subscription can appear in this view while requiring cancellation in the merchant’s own app.
- Do not expose account coordinates from the Info panel in a public support post.

## Cancel or reactivate a recurring payment

_Applies to: Mobile_

Cancel stops a Buburuza-managed recurring schedule. A deactivated schedule has a separate Activate route that can restart it immediately.

1. Open the intended recurring payment and review its recipient, amount and schedule.
2. Choose Cancel and read Are you sure?. Use No, keep the payment to leave it enabled, or Yes, cancel to confirm.
3. Check Recurring payment is cancelled and choose Close.
4. Find the record under Deactivated. Review its cancellation date, Total paid, Last paid and transaction history.
5. To restart it, choose Activate and read Reactivate the payment?. The warning says it starts right away.
6. Choose Keep disabled to leave it off, or Yes, reactivate only when you intend the immediate restart. Check the resulting schedule status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Cancellation of future scheduling is separate from reversing an already executed payment.
- Read the current reactivation and retention wording before cancelling.
- Do not reactivate merely to view payment history; history remains shown in the deactivated detail.

## Find where to cancel a merchant subscription

_Applies to: Mobile_

A merchant subscription listed in recurring payments can require cancellation through that merchant’s app.

1. Open the merchant subscription from Recurring payments.
2. Review its merchant, amount, frequency, payment method and history.
3. Choose Cancel. If Go to the app appears, read the instruction that cancellation must be completed in the merchant’s app and choose Okay.
4. Open the merchant’s official app and use its subscription-management process. Keep the merchant’s cancellation confirmation and check subsequent activity.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Dismissing Go to the app does not cancel the subscription.
- The merchant route differs from Yes, cancel on a Buburuza-managed payment schedule.
- The record’s End date can say Visit the app; do not infer an end date from it.

## Find a payment route or search for a person

_Applies to: Mobile_

Payments groups person-to-person transfers, crypto, bank transfers and other payment tools. Search is available when you want to find a Buburuza user.

1. Open Payments from the bottom navigation.
2. For a person, use Search name or phone or choose a contact from the Transfer to user carousel. Check the intended person before continuing.
3. For crypto, choose Send crypto or Receive crypto. For a bank transfer, choose the appropriate offered rail under Bank Transfers.
4. Use Between accounts to move or convert among your own balances, Scan to pay to begin with a document, or Request payment to ask someone to pay.
5. Use Recurring payments to manage schedules and Favorites to find a saved payment route.
6. If the search says Nothing found, use X to clear it or correct the name or phone number. Invite a friend starts an invitation route when needed.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A search match does not authorize a payment; confirm recipient identity and payment details in the subsequent flow.
- An invitation does not prove that the recipient already has an eligible account.
- If you need to use Check, open that payment method and review its current requirements before continuing.
