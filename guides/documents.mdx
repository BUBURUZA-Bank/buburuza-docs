## Choose the document that answers your question

| Document | Purpose shown in the web app |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly statement | Transactions and balances for a listed month. |
| Custom statement | Transactions and balances for a selected date range. |
| Bank letter | An account letter, separate from a statement or receiving instructions. |
| Wire details | Account and routing information for incoming wires. |
| Transaction receipt | A record attached to the specific payment. |
| Tax form | Tax information and certification on file, with add, update, download, or re-sign actions. |

Select the account before downloading. The Documents page states that statement dates use UTC and that account documents are generated with current account details. For a custom statement, use a date range permitted for the selected account.

The mobile document library may include additional categories. Keep their category names and actions distinct from the web statement and account-document controls.

## Choose the document type for the task

Use **Statements** for account activity over a period and **Receipts & attachments** for payment-related records. **Wire details** and **Bank letters** are separate account-document categories. **Notices** contains notices or agreements; **Billing History** contains invoice records.

**1099s** and **Recipient tax forms** are different categories. The recipient category describes W-9 or W-8BEN files uploaded by you or the recipient. The presence or absence of a category does not determine a person’s legal tax obligation.

**Other** is the entry point for adding an external document. Its upload choices include camera, photo library, files, email forwarding and manual entry. After adding a file, check the filename and metadata in the library before sharing it.

Filters follow the document type. Statements use year and accounts; receipts and attachments use a start/end period; billing uses year; tax documents use tax year. Applied chips let you see and clear the scope of the list.

## Find and download account statements

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Statements are available from the Documents area shown in Settings. Choose the period and account carefully before downloading a record.

1. Open Settings, then Documents, and choose the account on the web when the account selector is shown.
2. In Statements, choose the month from the year-grouped list and use its download action.
3. For a web statement covering another period, choose Custom date range.
4. In Custom statement, confirm the account and select the start and end dates within the permitted range shown.
5. Choose Download, then check the account and period on the resulting document.
6. Use the stated UTC time base when comparing statement dates with other transaction displays.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If a period is missing, check that you selected the correct account and contact support about availability.
- If a statement date differs from a date shown elsewhere, check the time-zone information displayed.

## Find a bank letter

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

The Documents area includes a Bank letters section. Use the document available for the relevant account and check that it meets the recipient’s requirements.

1. Open Settings, then Documents, and select the relevant account when the selector appears.
2. Open Bank letters. On the web this is listed under Account documents.
3. Use the download action for the letter.
4. Check the current account identity and document date. The web document area says account documents are generated with current account details.
5. If you need receiving wire instructions instead of an account letter, choose the separate Wire details document on the web.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the letter you need is not listed, contact support and describe the purpose and required information.
- Do not edit a bank letter yourself to add unsupported account or institution details.

## Find, filter, download and share mobile documents

_Applies to: Mobile_

The mobile Documents library groups statements, receipts, account documents and uploaded files. Filters differ by category.

1. Open Documents, including the Docs shortcut on Bank Home.
2. Choose All, Statements, Receipts & attachments, My documents, Wire details, Bank letters, Notices, Billing History, 1099s, Recipient tax forms or Other.
3. Use Search to find a file. Check its filename, associated account or document type, date or period, and file size.
4. For Statements, use Year and Apply. Open Accounts, select the intended fiat accounts or token/network entries within wallets, then choose Select.
5. For Receipts & attachments, open Period, enter Start date and End date, then choose Apply. Billing History uses Year; 1099s and Recipient tax forms use Tax year.
6. Check the applied filter chips. Use X on a chip to remove that filter when you want a broader list.
7. Open the file’s ellipsis menu, then choose Download or Share. Verify the selected file before sharing it.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- An empty category does not prove a missing document was deleted. Check category, account and date filters first.
- The Accounts filter supports several selections; Select is disabled when none is selected.
- Use the document’s Download or Share control for the action you intend.
- If you choose Generate, complete the options presented for that document type and check the resulting record.

## Find and review tax-document requests

_Applies to: Web_

The tax-document area shows documents on file and any action requested for your account. Follow the instructions for your account and ask a qualified adviser if you are unsure which tax information is correct.

1. Open Settings, then Tax, and inspect the document status: Required, On file, Expires soon, or Action needed.
2. If no form is on file, choose Add tax form. Review the form selected for the account and the pre-filled business details.
3. Complete the requested form fields. The W-9 interface includes federal tax classification and employer identification number; the W-8BEN-E interface includes entity type and tax ID, with a treaty-benefit control.
4. Read the full certification. Confirm that the entered information is correct only if you can make that certification.
5. Type the full legal name in Signature and choose Sign and submit. If the mobile signing screen uses Slide to sign, use that control and check Signed.
6. For an expiring form, choose Re-sign form, verify that the details remain accurate, and complete the signature step.
7. Use Download for a copy or Update when information needs changing. If the legal name or tax identifier is locked or a mismatch is reported, use the displayed Contact support route.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Do not guess tax classification, treaty eligibility, or certification answers. Obtain the appropriate professional guidance for the account.
- If the screen reports a name/taxpayer-ID mismatch, review the request before submitting an update.
- A completed signature and the document’s resulting on-file status should be checked separately.

## Download wire instructions for your account

_Applies to: Web_

The web Documents area offers wire details separately from statements and bank letters.

1. Open Settings, then Documents.
2. Choose the intended bank account.
3. Under Account documents, choose Wire details.
4. Download the available document.
5. Review its current account and routing information before sharing it with a sender.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Select the intended receiving account; a statement or bank letter is not a substitute for the specific wire instructions.
- If the document is missing or the details differ from the Receive screen, contact support before sharing conflicting instructions.

## Add a document to the mobile library

_Applies to: Mobile_

The Other category provides a place to start adding a document from several sources.

1. Open Documents and choose Other.
2. Choose Upload Document, including from the No other documents found empty state.
3. Choose Scan using camera, Upload from photo library, Choose from files, Forward by email or Enter manually, according to where the document is stored.
4. After completing the selected source’s upload journey in the app, return to Other and verify the expected filename, date and size before downloading or sharing it.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Choosing an upload method does not complete the upload. Check that the expected file appears in the document library.
- Check the upload form’s allowed file formats and size limit before selecting a file.
- Use the intended document only; remove sensitive material that is unrelated to the task before sharing.

## Find or request a recipient tax document on mobile

_Applies to: Mobile_

Recipient tax forms stores W-9 or W-8BEN files uploaded by you or the recipient, according to the library’s explanation.

1. Open Documents and choose Recipient tax forms.
2. Use Tax year to narrow the list, then choose Apply.
3. Check the matching file’s name and date before using its ellipsis menu to Download or Share.
4. If No recipient tax forms found appears, use Request to start the recipient-document request journey.
5. When inviting a recipient, use the tax-document request option if needed. For an existing recipient, use the available upload or request action.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The empty library does not decide which tax form a recipient is legally required to provide.
- A Request entry is separate from a delivered request or a received and validated document.
- The request form, reminder process and validation outcomes are not present in this library group.

## Complete the mobile W-9 information flow

_Applies to: Mobile_

The W-9 route collects your legal name, classification, taxpayer identifier, address and certifications. Use information that accurately describes your circumstances.

1. Open Tax Information Required from Home. Answer the U.S.-person question using your correct tax status. If you are unsure which form or classification applies, get qualified tax advice before signing.
2. In U.S. Tax Information (W-9), enter Full legal name as shown on the tax return. Add a Business or DBA name if applicable.
3. Choose the appropriate Tax Classification from the offered options: Individual/sole proprietor, Single-member LLC, C Corporation, S Corporation, Partnership, Trust/estate or Other. Choose Continue.
4. Choose the correct Tax ID Type, SSN or EIN, and enter the identifier in the format shown.
5. Complete Address (where you file taxes): Street, optional Apt/Suite, City, State and ZIP. Choose Continue.
6. Read every certification. Confirm only declarations that are accurate for you. Enter the matching full name in Electronic Signature and review Date.
7. When the form is accurate and you intend to sign it, choose Complete Tax Information. Check Tax Information Submitted, then choose Return home.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the SSN or EIN field reports an invalid format, check the selected identifier type and the displayed formatting requirements; never invent a number.
- If Signature must match the name provided appears, compare the signature with the name entered earlier.
- If the app asks you to check all certifications but a statement is not true for you, stop and resolve the issue rather than checking it to continue.
- Tax Information Submitted confirms the displayed submission state; it does not itself establish an issued tax document or independent tax acceptance.

## Complete the mobile foreign-individual tax-information flow

_Applies to: Mobile_

The foreign-individual route collects identity, residence, tax identification, any applicable treaty information and a signed certification.

1. Open Tax Information Required from Home. Answer the U.S.-person question using your correct tax status. If you are unsure which form or classification applies, get qualified tax advice before signing.
2. In Foreign Tax Information (W-8), choose Individual when that is the correct route for you, then Continue.
3. Enter Full legal name, Country of citizenship and the permanent residence address fields: Street, optional Apt, City, State/Province, Country and Postal Code.
4. Keep Same as permanent address selected if it is accurate for your mailing address. Otherwise clear it and complete the separate mailing address, then Continue.
5. On Tax Identification, enter the foreign tax identification number if applicable and your Date of birth, then Continue.
6. On Tax Treaty Benefits, answer whether you are claiming benefits based on your actual eligibility. If claiming, complete the applicable country, treaty article, withholding rate and income/explanation fields for your circumstances.
7. Read the certifications and confirm only accurate declarations. If signing for the account holder, state your capacity. Enter Electronic Signature and review Date.
8. When the form is accurate and you intend to sign it, choose Complete Tax Information. Check Tax Information Submitted, then choose Return home.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If the form rejects the residence address, check your actual country of residence and whether the selected form is appropriate. Do not enter an inaccurate address to bypass the error.
- If the date-of-birth check says you must be at least18, verify the entered date and seek help if the form cannot accurately represent your circumstances.
- Claiming treaty benefits is not simply a preference for a lower displayed rate; verify eligibility before completing those fields.
- Submission does not establish that a treaty claim was independently approved.

## Complete the mobile foreign-entity tax-information flow

_Applies to: Mobile_

The foreign-entity route asks for organization details, tax identifiers, FATCA classification, any applicable treaty claim and an authorized signature.

1. Open Tax Information Required from Home. Answer the U.S.-person question using your correct tax status. If you are unsure which form or classification applies, get qualified tax advice before signing.
2. In Foreign Tax Information (W-8), choose Business/Organization when that is the correct route, then Continue.
3. Enter Legal name of organization, Country where the entity is legally organized, any applicable disregarded-entity name and the correct Type of entity.
4. Complete the permanent residence address. Confirm Same as permanent address for mailing or enter the separate mailing Street, optional Suite, City, State/Province, Country and Postal Code.
5. On Tax Identification & FATCA, complete the applicable foreign tax ID and GIIN fields and choose the entity’s correct FATCA classification.
6. Answer the entity treaty-benefit question. If claiming benefits, complete the applicable Treaty Country, Treaty Article, Withholding Rate, Explanation and Limitation on Benefits fields.
7. Read the certifications. The authorized signer enters their name, title, Electronic Signature and Date, confirming only declarations that are accurate for the entity.
8. When the form is accurate and you intend to sign it, choose Complete Tax Information. Check Tax Information Submitted, then choose Return home.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Entity type, FATCA classification and treaty eligibility are different questions; obtain qualified advice if any classification is uncertain.
- Only a person authorized to sign for the entity should complete the signature step.
- Complete GIIN and classification fields according to the requirements that apply to your business.
- The displayed submission confirmation is separate from later tax reporting or receipt of year-end forms.

## Read account reports and letters

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Choose the report or letter that matches the information you need. Check the named account, reporting period, currency and issue date before relying on any document.

1. For a Statement, read each row’s Date, Description, Money Out, Money In and Balance. A description may identify a transfer, counterparty or masked card. Compare the reporting period with the transactions you want to check.
2. For Trades, compare Unique ID, Time (UTC), Pair, Type, Price, Cost, Volume, Fee and Margin. For Ledgers, compare Unique ID, Time (UTC), Type, Asset, Wallet, Amount, Fee and Resulting Balance. Use the identifier to distinguish records with similar amounts.
3. For a Portfolio report, check each currency or symbol, wallet, quantity, valuation price and total value, then the Total Account Balance. For an Account Statement, review the balances and Activity table separately; an empty section is different from a missing document.
4. For Domestic Transfer Details, check the receiving bank’s routing number, name and address, and the beneficiary name, account number, account type and address. For International Wire Details, also check SWIFT/BIC, IBAN or account number, any intermediary bank and required Remittance Information.
5. For a Profit / Earnings Report, check the reporting period and the account-by-account currency, opening date, closing date and profit or earnings entries. Keep the report with the records it summarizes.
6. For a Blockchain Address Certificate, compare the account-holder identity, blockchain address, reference and date. For a Bank Letter or Bank Account Confirmation Letter, compare the account-holder details, account identifiers, account type, opening date and current status where included.
7. For a Transfer Notice, check amount paid, transfer date, purpose, reference and payer/recipient details. For a Transfer Confirmation, also inspect amount received, creation date, status, fee and transfer method. Check the status explicitly before treating a transfer as completed.
8. For an Account Closure Certificate, check the account, closure-effective date, final posted balance and disposition of remaining funds. Match it to the account’s closure record before using it as evidence.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Use the receiving instructions issued for your own account and chosen payment method. Do not replace them with details from another account’s document.
- Check UTC timestamps and currency labels when comparing reports. Ask support to investigate if the document’s account, currency, amount or period does not match the corresponding record.
- A report section or column does not by itself establish that a service is enabled on your account. Review the actual records and account options available to you.
- A notice, confirmation title or certificate is not a substitute for reading its status, dates and account details. If a required field or document is unavailable, ask support about that specific requirement.
- A profits report does not determine tax treatment or third-party acceptance. Check the requirements of the person or organization requesting the document.
