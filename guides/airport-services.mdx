## Find airport lounges and access information

_Applies to: Mobile_

Airport services lets you find a lounge for your airport, read its access rules, and open the pass offered for your account.

1. Open Airport services from Home and choose Explore for an airport, or use the airport search by name or code.
2. Select Lounge and open the venue you intend to visit. Check its airport, terminal, gate and airside location.
3. Read the displayed operating hours, amenities, access rules, transport restrictions, booking and cancellation conditions. Rules can differ by service.
4. Choose Access lounge when it is offered. Check the membership/pass details and the guiding address shown for that venue.
5. Follow the access instructions: go to the lounge, display the required pass, and show a same-day boarding pass when the selected venue requires it.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A lounge listing does not by itself confirm that the venue is open, has capacity or accepts your current entitlement. Check the current access conditions.
- Check the price, child-age rules, maximum stay and cancellation cutoff for the particular lounge you plan to visit.
- If a pass is rejected or access instructions are unclear, seek help from the service provider or support using your booking reference.

## Review an airport fast-track booking

_Applies to: Mobile_

Fast-track booking has separate paid and membership entry paths. Review the service, traveller details, delivery choices and any charge before confirming.

1. Open Airport services, find the airport, select Fast track, and choose the arrival or departure service you need.
2. Read the operating hours, location and access conditions. Choose Book with membership when applicable, or Book for the displayed price.
3. In Visit information, choose the visit date and time and set the passenger count according to the selected service’s displayed age rules. Choose Next.
4. Enter the traveller’s full legal first/middle and last names, email and phone number. If you use the offered identity autofill, verify the details belong to the traveller. Choose Next.
5. In Checkout and delivery, check service, airport, location, guiding address, date/time window, passenger count and details. Choose Receive by email and/or Receive a QR-code in the app as offered.
6. Read the confirmation terms and any privacy or personal-information consent. Select the Confirmation checkbox only if you agree. Use Previous step to correct details, then confirm only when the final entitlement or charge is clear.
7. For the paid path, review Service, From and Amount on the Payment sheet and use its Pay control. Check for Successfully booked and the booking in Bookings.
8. Open the issued booking to access its QR and current location instructions. Present that booking pass at the indicated fast-track gate or lane.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Unavailable time slots may be grayed out. Choose an offered slot.
- Verify your membership entitlement and the final amount shown before booking. Do not assume a membership booking is free.
- If no email arrives, first check Bookings and the in-app QR delivery option. Do not pay again without checking the existing booking.
- The general membership pass is separate from an issued fast-track booking. Confirm the actual booking and use its QR.
- Arrival-time, child and cancellation rules are service-specific. Read the current booking instructions.
- If No results found appears for the selected airport, choose Change and search another airport. Availability must be checked for the airport and service you intend to use.

## Find an airport booking, pass, or cancellation option

_Applies to: Mobile_

Airport services separates upcoming Bookings, History, your membership pass and the QR for an individual fast-track booking.

1. Open Airport services and find the relevant entry under Bookings. Use History to find a previous service entry.
2. Open the booking and review its airport, service, visit time, passenger information and guiding address.
3. For fast-track access, use the QR in the booking that says to scan it at the Fast Track gates.
4. Use Show membership pass for membership identity, expiry and entitlement counters. This general pass is separate from the selected booking’s QR.
5. Choose Book again only when you want to start another booking.
6. If you need to cancel, read the selected service’s current cancellation conditions and open Cancel booking when available. Follow the actual confirmation shown and check the resulting booking status.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A checkout preview or membership QR is not proof that a specific booking succeeded. Look for the issued booking.
- Before using Cancel booking, read the current cancellation and refund terms. Confirm the outcome before expecting a refund or a restored entitlement.
- Check the traveller and visit details before confirming a repeat booking with Book again.

## Use your airport membership pass and check guest visits

_Applies to: Mobile_

The airport membership pass identifies your membership and shows separate cardholder and guest visit information. Check it alongside the selected service’s access rules.

1. Open Airport services and choose Show Buburuza pass or the membership-pass action shown for the selected lounge.
2. Check the membership QR, pass number and valid-until date. Read the lounge and fast-track counters for the cardholder and guests separately.
3. If the pass offers Refresh, use it when you need to refresh the displayed membership QR. Present the current QR at lounge reception according to the venue’s instructions.
4. Before bringing a guest, read the guest allowance and visit-use wording. Where the pass states that each guest uses one lounge visit allocation, check the remaining visits before entry.
5. For a booked fast-track visit, open the booking’s own QR and gate instructions. The general membership QR and a specific booking pass serve different purposes.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If Refresh does not produce a usable pass, check the membership details and ask support for help before assuming entry is available.
- Check the current validity and allowance counters; do not assume lounge and fast-track or cardholder and guest allowances are interchangeable.
- Presenting a membership QR does not guarantee lounge capacity or entry. Check the venue’s current access conditions.
