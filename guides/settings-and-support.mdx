## Choose support or feedback

Use **Get help** or **Support** when you need a response about your account, a payment, verification, or another problem. The web form offers a topic and message, and says the team will reply by email.

Use **Feedback** or **Share feedback** for a product idea, something missing, or an experience you want the team to consider. The feedback page directs customers who need an answer to Support instead.

For a payment problem, include the action you tried, current status or error, and the relevant transaction reference. Mask sensitive account details. Keep authentication codes, passcodes, recovery information, and full card details out of both forms.

## Update your profile or company information

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Open your profile to see which details you can edit directly. Some verified identity or company fields may need support to change them.

1. Open Settings or Profile.
2. Choose the profile or company-information area.
3. Select an editable field and enter the corrected information.
4. Complete any verification prompt required by the app and save.
5. For a locked field, use Get help and explain which detail needs updating.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If an email or phone change asks for a verification code, enter that code only in the app.
- Do not send identity documents or full account details in an initial support request. Support can direct you to the appropriate secure process.

## Manage notification preferences

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Notification settings let you review the updates offered for your account. Some security notices may remain enabled even if other notifications are turned off.

1. Open Settings, then Notifications.
2. On mobile, inspect Allow App notifications and Allow Email notifications and choose the channels you want.
3. In the detailed mobile Profile layout, choose Notifications. Set In-app messages (News, Updates, Changes to account info), Push notifications (Transactions, Changes to account info) and E-mail subscription (News, New features, System maintenance) separately.
4. On the web, inspect Money received, Money sent, Payment issues, Verification updates, and Product news separately.
5. Review New login and Security changes in the security section. The web screen labels security alerts as always on.
6. For a missing mobile alert, also check the device’s notification permission. For an email alert, check the address and mail filtering.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If an expected alert does not arrive, check both the account preference and your device or email settings.
- Turning off an optional notification does not change a transaction’s status or remove a payment obligation.

## Change mobile appearance, language and display currency

_Applies to: Mobile_

Appearance controls the mobile theme, preferred display options and app icon. These display choices do not move money between accounts.

1. Open Profile, then Appearance.
2. Choose System, Light or Dark for the theme.
3. Open Language and select from the choices actually available.
4. Open Currency display to choose the currency used for presentation.
5. Review the app icon choices and select the available style you prefer.
6. Return to the app to check the appearance. Use the separate Home widgets guide for widget controls.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- System appearance can follow the device theme. If your screen looks different, check this setting.
- Changing Currency display does not convert a balance or submit a transfer.

## Review an updated Terms of Service notice

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

When Buburuza asks you to accept updated terms, the notice links to the changed documents and explains the effect on sending money. The shown notice allows receiving to continue while sending is paused until acceptance.

1. Open Accept the updated terms, or choose Review from the Sending is paused banner.
2. Open and read the linked Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and check the update information.
3. Choose Accept and continue if you agree.
4. Choose Not now if you need to return later. The notice explains that sending stays paused while acceptance is outstanding.
5. Return to the account and check the banner or sending state. Use support if you need clarification about the documents.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If an action is unavailable, check whether the terms notice is still outstanding.
- Do not assume dismissing the notice has recorded acceptance. Check the account state after any action you take.

## Request help closing your account

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

The mobile and web closure screens use different processes. Read the process for the platform you are using and settle questions about remaining funds before completing an irreversible confirmation.

1. On the web, open Settings, then Profile, and find Close your account.
2. Review the instruction to withdraw the remaining balance, then choose Contact support to close and follow the account-specific process.
3. On mobile, open Settings and choose Delete account.
4. Read the full deletion warning. The warning says the account cannot be recovered or used afterward and that any assets left in the wallet become inaccessible.
5. Choose Continue only after addressing the warning. Enter the email code in the app if you decide to proceed.
6. Read the final Delete Forever confirmation and complete it only when you intend to delete the account. Keep the resulting confirmation or contact support if the final state is unclear.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Do not assume deleting the mobile app closes your financial account.
- If you see a self-service deletion option, read the confirmation carefully and ask support about unresolved funds or activity before proceeding.

## Contact support about an app problem

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Use the help option in Buburuza or the official Help Center when you need assistance. A clear description helps support locate the issue without unnecessary sensitive information.

1. Open Get help or the Help area in Settings.
2. Choose the relevant topic if the form offers one.
3. Describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and the exact error message.
4. For a transaction issue, include the date, amount, currency, payment method, and reference with sensitive details masked.
5. Add a redacted screenshot only if it helps explain the problem, then submit the request through the official form.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Never include a password, app passcode, verification code, recovery code, private key, seed phrase, or full payment-card details.
- If support needs identity or financial documents, follow the secure upload instructions provided for your case.

## Send product feedback

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Use feedback to share an idea or describe a rough edge. Use support when you need an answer about an account or payment.

1. On mobile, open Settings and choose Share feedback. On the web, open Settings and choose Feedback.
2. Describe the idea, missing feature, or experience you want to explain.
3. Choose the feedback submission action.
4. Read the confirmation. If the matter needs an account-specific response, open Get help or Support instead.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- The web feedback screen explicitly directs customers who need an answer to Support.
- Do not include passwords, security codes, full payment details, or identity documents in a feedback message.

## Change the email address in your mobile profile

_Applies to: Mobile_

Change your email in the mobile Account settings, then verify the new address using the link described in the app.

1. Open Profile, then Account and E-mail.
2. Enter your app passcode when requested.
3. Enter the new email address and choose Verify. Check the address before sending the verification link.
4. Open the verification link sent to that address. If needed, use Request another one when the displayed countdown ends.
5. Return to Account and check New e-mail is set! and the updated address. If your app shows Personal info, start the change with Change beside Email and use Continue.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Check the new inbox and mail filtering if the link does not arrive.
- An email change link is different from a sign-in verification code.
- Never share the verification link or your app passcode with another person.

## Change the phone number in your mobile profile

_Applies to: Mobile_

Update your mobile phone number and verify that you can receive the confirmation code at the new number.

1. Open Profile, then Account and Phone number. Enter your app passcode when requested.
2. Choose the country code and enter your new phone number.
3. On the delivery chooser, choose Send SMS for text-message verification.
4. Enter the OTP in the number of boxes displayed. This mobile layout shows four boxes; the earlier Personal info route uses six.
5. Use Request another one only when the displayed countdown allows it.
6. Check New phone number is set! and the updated number on Account.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Check the country code and number before requesting the code.
- Link Telegram/WhatsApp is an alternative entry in the delivery chooser; use it only if its actual linking instructions are available in your app.
- A sign-in code and a phone-change code serve different actions. Never send either to support.

## Update your mobile identity details or nickname

_Applies to: Mobile_

Identity information and a nickname are separate settings. Changes to legal details can require supporting documents.

1. Open Profile, then Account. Choose Identity details for legal information or Nickname for your display handle.
2. For Identity details, enter your app passcode when requested. Review legal first/middle names, last names, date of birth, country of residence, state, city, address lines and phone number.
3. Enter the changes and choose Save. Read the notice that the information must match your official documents and that you will need to provide proof.
4. Choose Yes, change to proceed with that request, or Cancel to return without confirming it. Follow any document request actually provided by the app.
5. For Nickname, enter the new value in the @ field and choose Save.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A submitted identity change is not proof that the new information has completed verification.
- The nickname form does not establish that every handle is available or permitted.
- Log out and Delete account are separate Account entries; changing profile details does not trigger those actions.

## Report a problem from the mobile app

_Applies to: Mobile_

The Report a problem form lets you describe the affected area and add supporting images or PDFs.

1. Open Profile, then Help and Report a problem.
2. Choose Section: Account, Home, Payments, Chats or Other.
3. Describe the issue in What happened? Include what you were doing and what appeared on screen.
4. If a supporting file is useful, choose Add in Attachments, then Upload from photo library or Choose from files. The form accepts .png, .pdf and .jpeg files up to 5 MB.
5. Review attached filenames and sizes. Use the attachment menu’s Edit or Delete control if needed.
6. Choose Send when enabled and look for Report is sent.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Send is disabled in the empty form. Check the section and description before submitting.
- If an attachment cannot be added, check the listed file type and size requirements.
- Do not include passwords, OTPs, PINs, private keys or recovery phrases in the report.

## Find app updates and product information

_Applies to: Mobile_

Profile provides release announcements, app-update navigation and links to company information and documents.

1. Open Profile and choose Updates to read the dated release and announcement cards.
2. Use Update app on the release card when available.
3. If the Updates screen says in-app update notifications are disabled, choose Go to Notifications to review that preference.
4. Open Profile, then About us for Licences, Privacy policy and Terms of service entries, plus Website, X and Instagram links.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- An announcement card is different from a transaction status or support reply.
- Use the actual linked legal document for its contents; a menu entry alone does not establish a licence or contractual term.

## Review mobile balance privacy and travel settings

_Applies to: Mobile_

Privacy & security includes Transaction privacy and Travel mode alongside authentication settings.

1. Open Profile, then Privacy & security.
2. Review Transaction privacy. Its helper describes hiding balances by default; choose the setting you want.
3. Review Travel mode and the explanation currently shown before changing it.
4. Return to the app to check the balance presentation.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- Hiding a displayed balance does not change the balance or a payment status.
- Travel mode does not establish that a transaction will bypass geographic, banking or security restrictions.

## Read phone widgets and use their quick actions

_Applies to: Mobile_

Buburuza widgets can show compact account information and shortcuts. Check the information in the app when you need the full balance, transfer record or payment review.

1. On an available balance widget, check Total balance and the displayed currency or token equivalent. Use the eye control when you want to change whether the balance is visible.
2. Use the spending widget to review Today spending and its category bars. Quote widgets show a compact asset list; open the app for fuller asset information.
3. Choose the available payment shortcut: Pay by QR-code, By phone, By crypto or Own account. Continue through the corresponding recipient, amount and review steps in the app.
4. On a B1 widget, use Tap to ask B1 or the microphone control. Watch for Listening and read the assistant’s response before taking a separate account action.
5. For an ongoing transfer, compare its amount, asset or network, sent time, ETA and current stage. Created, Processing and Completed identify different progress states.
6. In the app, open Ongoing Transfers when you need to compare multiple transfers. For a Failed item, use Check details; close a completed status card with its X when available.
7. For a flight card, read the flight identifier, departure and arrival locations and times. Check the booking and carrier information separately before travelling.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If a shortcut opens the app, finish the payment review there. A shortcut or B1 response does not show that money has been sent.
- An ETA is an estimate. Check the transfer’s own status and details if its progress changes or the payment fails.
- For an Add money to loan collateral warning, open the loan and review its collateral information and top-up action. Read the loan’s current terms and warning before acting.
- If a balance, quote or flight time needs confirmation, check the corresponding app record or service. Do not rely on a compact widget alone for a payment or travel decision.
