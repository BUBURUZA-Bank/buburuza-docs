## Add a recipient and payment details

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

A recipient stores the person or business you want to pay and the payment details you will use. Review those details carefully before saving or sending.

1. Open Recipients and choose Add recipient.
2. Choose Person or Business. Enter Legal name for a person or Legal business name for a business, matching the name on the receiving bank account.
3. Complete Beneficiary name and the address fields requested. Email is for payment receipts; Nickname is a private reference for identifying the recipient.
4. Choose Add payment method to save bank details or a wallet address now. If the web form offers it, you can create the recipient and add payment details later.
5. Choose Create recipient, then inspect the saved recipient profile.
6. Before paying, choose the correct saved payment method and verify its bank details or asset, network, and address.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If a field is marked invalid, correct it before continuing. For crypto, verify the selected network as well as the address.
- If the recipient changes their payment instructions, confirm the change through a trusted channel before updating the saved details.

## Edit or remove a saved recipient

_Applies to: Mobile and web_

Keep saved recipient information current so that future payments use the intended destination. Review a change before making the next payment.

1. Open Recipients and select the person or business.
2. Choose the edit action for the profile or payment details you need to change.
3. Update the information and save.
4. If you want to remove the recipient, choose Delete and review the confirmation before completing it.
5. Check the recipient and payment method on the next payment’s review screen.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- If a field cannot be changed, use Get help to ask how to update it.
- Editing or deleting a saved recipient should not be treated as cancelling a transfer that has already been submitted. Check that transaction separately.

## Find and start a saved payment from Favorites

_Applies to: Mobile_

Favorites brings recent and saved payment entries into one place. Opening an entry starts its payment route; it does not itself send money.

1. Open Favorites from Payments.
2. Use Search, Recent payments or the saved list to find the intended entry. Check the payment type shown beside it, such as To Bubu user, Wire, Ethereum or Personal fiat account.
3. Open the intended saved entry, then check the recipient and payment details in the route that follows.
4. To start adding an entry, choose Add Payment. Select Bank transfer, Crypto transfer or Payment for services from What do you want to add?
5. Continue only through the actual form offered, reviewing details and the final payment before authorizing.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A favorite can represent a payment route or account, not only a person.
- Payment for services is an entry for categories such as internet and utilities. Check the provider and payment instructions after selecting a category; if these are missing or unclear, contact support before paying.
- Do not assume tapping a favorite has completed a transfer.

## Add a person or business for a payment request

_Applies to: Mobile_

The payment-request recipient form separates legal identity and address from a nickname used only inside your account.

1. From Request payment, choose Create or Create recipient.
2. Select Person or Business. Enter Recipient’s legal name for a person, or Recipient name for a business.
3. Enter Email if you will need email delivery. Nickname is optional and is described as private to you.
4. Choose Add recipient’s address. Enter Legal address, Country, City, State/Province/Region and Postal/ZIP code. Apt/Suite/Floor is optional.
5. Choose Done and check the address summary. Use its edit pencil if a field needs correction.
6. If Request tax documents is relevant, review that option before selecting it. It starts a separate document-request process.
7. Choose Create when the required fields are complete. Find the recipient in the saved list by legal/display name and private nickname.
8. Use the saved-list Search separately from the top Buburuza contact search. Open the intended recipient to continue the payment request.

**Troubleshooting and important distinctions**

- A filled name without the required address still leaves Create disabled in the shown form.
- A nickname helps you recognize the recipient and is not visible to them.
- Swiping a saved-recipient row reveals X. Review any confirmation before removing the recipient and check the resulting list.
- Requesting a tax document is separate from receiving or validating it.
