# Onboarding & Security

## Signup

Accounts are created with an **email address**. There is no password: Buburuza is **passwordless by design**.

1. Enter your email on the signup form.
2. Confirm the one-time code sent to that address.
3. Set up your primary credential — a **passkey** (WebAuthn). If your device does not support passkeys, a TOTP authenticator is offered as a fallback.
4. Save your **recovery codes**. They are the only way back in if you lose every credential.

## Organisation onboarding (KYB)

After signup you onboard your **organisation** through a guided step machine:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Legal & company name">
    Provide the legal representative's name and the company name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document checklist">
    Review the list of documents required for your jurisdiction (Canada or US).
  </Step>
  <Step title="KYB verification">
    Complete identity verification for the company and its associated persons. Verification is handled by a licensed identity provider (Sumsub) in a hosted flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment account">
    When KYB passes, your organisation's **payment account is provisioned automatically** — nothing to do. You can watch the status while it activates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provider agreement (if required)">
    Depending on the payment provider, you may be asked to accept hosted terms in an embedded frame before money movement is enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can pause and resume onboarding at any time; your progress is saved per step.

<Tip>
KYB is the gate for money movement. Until it passes, your account is in verification and cannot hold or move funds.
</Tip>

## Signing in

- **Passkey login** — one touch, no codes. Works across your devices via platform sync.
- **Email login** — a one-time code, useful on new devices.
- **MFA (optional)** — you can enable an additional TOTP step for logins in settings.
- **Recovery codes** — single-use codes printed at signup; keep them somewhere safe.

## Session & device management

- Every sign-in creates a **session** you can review and revoke individually or all at once.
- Sessions are kept short-lived and refreshed automatically while you use the app.
- Logging out everywhere invalidates every active session.

## Security model

| Control | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Passwordless credentials | Passkeys (WebAuthn) as primary; TOTP as fallback and for optional MFA |
| One-time codes | Email OTP for signup confirmation and email login |
| Recovery codes | Single-use, issued at signup |
| Session management | List, revoke one, revoke all; short-lived access tokens with cookie-based refresh |
| Verification | KYB/KYC by a licensed identity provider (Sumsub) |
| Monitoring | Continuous transaction monitoring and travel-rule reporting |
