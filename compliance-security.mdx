# Compliance & Security

## Verification (KYB/KYC)

Every organisation must pass **Know Your Business (KYB)** verification before money movement is enabled:

- Verification is performed by a **licensed identity provider** (Sumsub) in a hosted flow.
- The required document set depends on the jurisdiction (currently **Canada and the US**).
- Organisations verify at a tier: **light** or **full** verification. The tier gates the products and limits available to the account.

Associated persons (directors, controllers, UBOs) are verified as part of the company verification.

## Transaction monitoring

All money movement is monitored continuously:

- **KYT** — counterparty and transaction screening against sanction and watch lists.
- **Travel Rule** — information sharing on transfers above applicable thresholds, as required by regulation.
- **Reporting** — transaction reporting to regulators (e.g. FINTRAC obligations for Canadian activity).

Monitoring is automatic; nothing is required from you beyond completing verification and keeping your organisation's information current.

## Account security

| Control | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Passkeys | WebAuthn credentials as the primary login factor — resistant to phishing and credential stuffing |
| MFA | Optional TOTP step-up for logins |
| Recovery codes | Single-use codes issued at signup for credential loss |
| Session control | List and revoke sessions, log out everywhere |
| Verification limits | Money movement disabled until KYB passes; tier-based product gating |

## Data & privacy

- We do not expose raw provider payloads or verification internals through any API.
- Account data is scoped to your organisation; requests are authorised per session.
- See [Onboarding & Security](/onboarding-and-security) for the user-facing flow.

## API notes

Compliance state surfaces through the API as statuses (KYB status, payment-account status) and standard error codes (`403` when a tier or status blocks an operation). The API never returns provider identifiers, raw evidence, or internal screening records.
