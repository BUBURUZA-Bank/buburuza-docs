<Note>
  This guide describes the Concierge preview. Requests, proposals, and
  confirmations use simulated responses. Sending or confirming a request does
  not contact a live concierge, arrange a booking, or make a payment.
</Note>

## Open Concierge

Sign in and choose **Concierge** from the main navigation. The landing page
introduces requests such as travel, dining, and everyday assistance.

Choose **New request** to start, or **View my requests** to open the inbox. If the
feature is not enabled, the navigation item is hidden and direct links return
you to **Dashboard**.

## Create a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe what you need">
    Click **New request** on the landing page or in **Your requests**.

    In **What do you need?**, enter a short summary, such as an airport transfer
    or restaurant request. In **Request details**, add the locations,
    preferences, and other information needed to understand it. Both fields
    are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a date and time">
    Open **When do you need it?** and select a day. Open **Time** to choose the
    hour and minute. Check the complete date and time shown in the field before
    sending.

    The time must still be in the future when you submit. Choosing a day before
    choosing a time initially sets it to **00:00**. For a request later today,
    select a later time as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Click **Send request**. The fields are unavailable while the request is
    being submitted.

    After a successful response, the app opens **Request sent** with your
    request title and an **In progress** badge. Choose **View requests** to
    return to the inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If submission fails, your entries stay in the form so you can correct them or
retry. They are not a saved draft and are not guaranteed to survive leaving the
page or reloading it.

## Check the date and time

The picker updates the selected value as you choose a day, hour, or minute;
there is no separate date confirmation button. Close the picker after checking
the selected value.

You can use the time wheel with a keyboard: **Tab** moves between its hour and
minute columns, **Up Arrow** and **Down Arrow** change the selected value, and
**Home** or **End** selects the first or last value in that column.

Dates and times are formatted in your interface language and your device's
local time zone. The selected time is submitted with its UTC offset. The inbox
and proposal also use the viewer's time zone, which may differ from the venue's.
Check that the displayed time represents the instant you intend.

## Follow your requests

Open **View my requests** from Concierge. Each inbox row shows the request title,
scheduled date and time, a subtitle when available, its status, and its last
update date. Select a request to open it.

| Status | What the preview shows | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In progress** | The request has been created and opens on **Request sent**. | Return to **View requests** to check its current state. |
| **Proposal ready** | A proposal with a title, description, and total price. | Review it and choose **Confirm proposal**. |
| **Confirmed** | A confirmation screen with the schedule and a reference when supplied. | Review the confirmation or choose **Back to requests**. |

When there are no requests, the inbox shows **No requests yet** and a
**New request** action. The current inbox has no **Load more** or next-page
control, so it displays only the first page of requests returned.

Creating a request does not automatically generate a proposal in the preview.
The sample requests include a proposal-ready example for trying confirmation.

## Review and confirm a proposal

Open a request marked **Proposal ready**. Review its scheduled date and time,
proposal title, description, and **Total**, including the displayed currency.
The current screen presents one proposal; it has no selector for competing
options.

Click **Confirm proposal** and wait for the response. On success, the detail
screen changes to **Concierge booking confirmed**, the status becomes
**Confirmed**, and a **Reference** appears when provided. Choose **Back to
requests** to return to the updated inbox.

If the proposal is no longer awaiting confirmation, the app tells you and
refreshes the request. Read the updated state before attempting another action.
A failed confirmation is not shown as a successful booking.

In this delivery, the confirmation and reference are simulated. The screen does
not provide a payment method, checkout, or proposal expiry time.

## Questions and changes

**Ask a question** currently displays **This isn't available yet.** It does not
send a message or open a conversation.

Although some screen text refers to a thread, later replies, or notifications,
this delivery has no conversation composer or notification flow. It also has no
controls to edit or cancel a submitted request or change a confirmed booking.
Include the necessary details in the initial request while exploring the preview.

## Resolve an error

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **This field is required.** | Complete the indicated summary, details, or date/time field. |
| **Choose a time that hasn't passed yet.** | Select a later date and time. A time can pass while the form remains open. |
| **Check this value and try again.** | Review the highlighted field, correct its value, and send again. |
| Another submission error | Read the message and retry when appropriate. Your entries remain on the current page. |
| **Unable to load requests** or **Unable to load this request** | Click **Try again**. |
| **Request not found** | Choose **Back to requests** and open an available request from the inbox. |
| A proposal is no longer waiting for confirmation | Let the request refresh and review its current status. |
| **Ask a question** is unavailable | Messaging is not implemented in this preview. |
