<Note>
  This guide describes the Cards preview. It uses simulated cards and responses;
  it does not issue usable payment cards or change live card payments.
</Note>

## Open Cards

Select your space and choose **Cards** in the main navigation. If the feature
is not enabled, the navigation entry is hidden and a direct link returns you
to **Dashboard**.

Cards are grouped by cardholder. Each row shows the card name, **Physical** or
**Virtual** type, last four digits, and spending this month. A **Frozen** badge
identifies a frozen card. If a monthly limit is set, it appears beside spending.

When there are no cards, the page shows **No cards yet** and an **Issue card**
action. A loading error is shown separately, with **Try again**.

## Open a card

Select a card to open its details drawer. The drawer shows spending this month,
the displayed reset date, and the card's status. Under **Details**, you can review
its type, linked account, cardholder, billing address, expiry, and creation date.

The list can contain physical and virtual cards. The **Issue card** dialog
creates virtual cards only; it has no physical-card ordering flow.

## Actions available to your role

In the current preview, owners and admins can issue cards, reveal card numbers,
freeze or unfreeze cards, rename them, and change their limits. Viewers can read
the list and ordinary details, but these actions are locked, including freezing
a card assigned to themselves. The production permissions are still awaiting
confirmation.

## Show and hide card details

The full card number and CVV are hidden when the drawer opens. The last four
digits and expiry remain visible; expiry can be copied without revealing the
other values.

Click **Show details** to reveal the full card number and CVV. Their copy buttons
become available. A successful copy shows **Copied**; a failed copy shows
**Could not copy**.

The revealed number and CVV hide automatically after **60 seconds**. Click
**Hide details** to hide them sooner. Closing the drawer or switching to another
card also hides them. Reopening the card starts with its details hidden again.
The expiry remains visible after hiding.

## Freeze or unfreeze a card

Click **Freeze card** in an active card's drawer. After the request succeeds,
**Card frozen** appears, the list and drawer show **Frozen**, and the action
changes to **Unfreeze card**.

Click **Unfreeze card** to reverse the preview state. After success, **Card
unfrozen** appears and the frozen badge is removed. A failed request displays
an error instead of a success message.

These actions change the simulated card state. Their effect on real payments
has not been implemented in this delivery.

## Rename a card

Under **Details**, click **Rename**. Enter a non-empty name of up to **24
characters**, then click **Save**. After success, **Card name saved** appears and
the new name is shown in both the drawer and list.

Click **Cancel** to leave the current name unchanged.

## Manage the card's monthly spending limit

The **Spending limit** section belongs to the selected card. It shows the amount
spent against its limit, or **No spending limit** when no card limit is set.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the limit editor">
    Click **Set limit** if there is no limit, or **Edit** beside an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a monthly amount">
    Enter an amount in the currency displayed. It must be greater than zero and
    at least the amount this card has already spent this month. The editor shows
    the amount already spent and explains invalid values beside the field.

    **Save** stays disabled for an empty or invalid amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Click **Save**. After success, **Spending limit saved** appears and both the
    drawer and list reflect the updated limit. Click **Cancel** to leave it
    unchanged.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove an existing limit, open **Edit** and click **Remove**. After success,
**Spending limit removed** appears and the drawer shows **No spending limit**.
Entering zero or clearing the amount does not remove a limit.

The displayed reset date belongs to this card's monthly spending summary.
Removing a card limit does not establish unlimited spending or remove other
account restrictions. Live limit enforcement is outside this preview.

## Issue a virtual card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start issuance">
    Click **Issue card** in the page header or empty state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a cardholder">
    Select an active team member under **Cardholder**. If there is exactly one
    active member, the preview selects them automatically.

    While team members are loading, issuance is unavailable. If loading fails,
    use **Try again**. If there are no active members, add one before issuing a
    card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set optional details">
    Optionally enter a **Card name** of up to 24 characters and a **Monthly limit**.
    The issuance preview uses USD and accepts up to two decimal places. A limit
    must be greater than zero; leave it blank to create the card without a
    monthly card limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue and review">
    Click **Issue card** and wait for the request to finish. The dialog stays
    open while the request is pending.

    On success, **Virtual card issued** appears, the dialog closes, and the new
    card's drawer opens. The card appears under its cardholder in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An issuance error leaves the dialog open with your entries so you can correct
them or retry. **Cancel** closes it without another submission when no request
is pending. This flow creates a simulated card only.

## Resolve an error

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Cards could not be loaded** | Click **Try again** to reload the list. |
| **Card details could not be loaded** | Use **Try again** when offered, or **Back to cards** to return to the list. A card-not-found response offers no retry. |
| A card action is locked | Your viewer role cannot perform that action in the preview. Ask an owner or admin. |
| **That didn't work** | Read the action error in the drawer and retry when appropriate. |
| A limit must be greater than zero | Enter a positive amount. Use **Remove** to remove an existing limit. |
| A limit is below this month's spending | Enter at least the amount already spent, as shown in the editor. |
| A rename or limit update fails | Read the error beside the field, correct the value if needed, and save again. |
| No cardholders are available | Wait for team loading, retry a failed request, or add an active team member. |
