<Note>
  This guide describes the spending limit preview. It does not announce the
  feature's availability or confirm how limits affect live payments.
</Note>

Use **Spending limit** to review the monthly limit, the amount already spent,
and the remaining allowance for your selected space.

## Open your spending limit

Select the space you want to review. Open **Settings**, then choose
**Spending limit** under **Payments**.

If **Spending limit** is missing, the feature is not enabled. Opening its address
directly returns you to your profile settings.

The preview keeps a separate limit for each company space and for your personal
space. Check the selected space before making a change.

## Read the monthly summary

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly limit** | The current limit in the currency shown on the page. |
| **Spent since** | Spending counted from the displayed start date. |
| **Remaining** | The limit minus spending so far. This never displays a negative amount. |
| Progress bar | The proportion used, up to a full bar when the limit is reached or exceeded. |
| **Resets** | The start of the next monthly period. |

The preview uses calendar months. Follow the dates displayed on the page.
If spending reaches or exceeds the limit, the remaining allowance is zero and
the page shows a limit-reached message.

## Who can change the limit

Owners and admins can edit the limit. Viewers can read the summary, but
**Edit** is locked. If you are a viewer, ask an owner or admin to make the change.

## Change your monthly limit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click **Edit** beside **Monthly limit**. The dialog starts with your current
    limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a new amount">
    Enter an amount greater than zero, in the currency shown. Use the supported
    number of decimal places. If the amount exceeds your permitted maximum,
    the field displays the maximum you can enter.

    **Save** stays disabled if the amount is invalid or unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Click **Save** to submit the new amount. A lower limit applies after the save
    succeeds, without an additional identity confirmation. A higher limit
    requires an additional identity confirmation. Follow the confirmation
    prompt shown by the app, such as a passkey prompt.

    If you enter a limit below the amount already spent, the dialog warns you.
    Lowering the limit does not erase spending already counted for this month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    After a successful save, the dialog closes and you see **Spending limit
    saved**. Review the updated limit and remaining allowance.

    To leave the amount unchanged before submitting, click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An error instead of the monthly summary | Click **Try again** to load the limit. |
| An amount must be greater than zero | Enter a positive amount in the format accepted by the field. |
| An amount exceeds the maximum | Enter an amount at or below the maximum shown in the error. |
| **Edit** is locked | Ask an owner or admin to change the limit. |
| A higher limit cannot be confirmed yet | The requested increase was not saved. Keep the existing limit until confirmation becomes available. |
| Another error while saving | Read the message in the dialog and resolve it before trying again. |

If a background refresh fails after the summary has loaded, the page keeps the
last loaded values. Those values may not reflect a more recent change.

## When the limit is reached

A full progress bar and zero remaining allowance describe the displayed usage.
They do not confirm that a payment has been sent, queued, or scheduled to retry.
Check the payment's own status in **Transactions**. Automatic holding or retrying
of payments is not established by this preview.
