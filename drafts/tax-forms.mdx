<Note>
  This guide describes the company tax form preview. Live submission, signed
  document downloads, and the full certification are not available in this
  delivery. A successful preview submission is not a live tax filing.
</Note>

Use **Tax** to check the form recorded for your selected company and follow the
available action. The form type and status shown on the page come from the
company record; you do not choose between forms in the dialog.

## Open your tax settings

Select a company space, open **Settings**, and choose **Tax** under **Company**.
This section is not available in personal spaces.

If the feature is not enabled, **Tax** is hidden and opening its address directly
returns you to your profile settings.

When the company has **No form on file**, the Dashboard can show a reminder near
the balance card. Choose **Add**, or tap the reminder icon on a touch screen, to
open **Tax**. This reminder does not appear for the other form statuses.

## Understand your form status

| Status | What the preview shows | Available action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No form on file** | A **Required** badge and a request to add a form. | **Add tax form** |
| **On file** | The form type, signing date, and signer when available. A W-8BEN-E also shows its valid-through date when available. | **Update** |
| **Expires soon** | An expiry warning, with a date when available. | **Re-sign form**, or **Update** if details have changed. |
| **Action needed** | A message asking you to correct or update the form. | **Update form** |

Use the dates and message shown for your company. **On file** describes the
record in the app; it is not confirmation that the IRS has accepted a form.

## Add a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Add tax form**. The preview opens the W-8BEN-E or W-9 associated with
    your company record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the required fields">
    For a **W-8BEN-E**, select **Entity type** and enter the full **Tax ID number**.
    **Claim a tax treaty benefit** is shown only when the company record allows
    that option; its presence does not establish entitlement to a benefit.

    For a **W-9**, select **Federal tax classification** and enter the full
    nine-digit **Employer Identification Number**. The preview accepts the EIN
    with or without the hyphen after its first two digits. It has no separate
    SSN entry flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the confirmation and signature">
    The certification checkbox and **Signature** field start empty each time
    you open the dialog. The preview requires you to check the certification
    and type your full legal name before **Sign and submit** is enabled.

    **Read the full certification** currently displays **Full certification is
    not available yet.** It does not open a document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and review the result">
    Click **Sign and submit**. Wait for the request to finish; the dialog cannot
    be closed while submission is pending.

    On success, the dialog closes and a message confirms that the form was
    completed and signed. The page refreshes its status and signing details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The preview does not provide a full review of the company data and certification
being signed. Complete certification review remains a requirement before this
flow can be published for live use.

## Update a form

Click **Update** beside the form, or **Update form** in an **Action needed** alert.
The existing classification or entity type is selected when available.

Stored tax IDs are shown only in masked form. The tax ID or EIN input starts
empty: enter the full number again, not the masked value. The certification
and signature also start empty.

Make your changes and click **Sign and submit**. A successful update replaces
the form on file and refreshes its signing details. For changes to the company's
legal name or tax identifier, the page also provides **Contact support**.

## Re-sign an expiring form

For **Expires soon**, click **Re-sign form**. This dialog is for signing the
existing form again without editing its details. If details need to change,
use **Update** instead.

The re-sign dialog asks for a fresh certification and your full legal name in
**Signature**. Click **Sign and submit**. After a successful response, the dialog
closes and the page refreshes the signing and validity information. This dialog
does not ask you to re-enter the tax ID.

## Download a form

**Download** is visible for a form on file, but currently displays **Download is
not available yet.** No PDF is downloaded. Completing or re-signing the form in
the preview does not enable this action.

## Resolve an error

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| An error while loading the form | Click **Try again**. A loading failure does not mean the company has no form. |
| **Sign and submit** is disabled | Complete the required fields, certification, and signature. Wait if a request is already pending. |
| A required-field message | Fill in the field identified by the message. |
| A masked tax ID or EIN is rejected | Enter the full number instead of the value containing asterisks. |
| An EIN format error | Enter nine digits, with an optional hyphen after the first two. |
| A field is rejected after submission | Correct the highlighted field and submit again. |
| A submission or service error | Read the message and retry when appropriate. Your entries remain in the open dialog. |

Before submitting, **Cancel** closes the dialog without saving. Entries retained
after an error are not a saved draft and should not be expected to survive
closing the dialog or reloading the page.
