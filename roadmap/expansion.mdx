## Geographic expansion

Buburuza enters three major markets simultaneously, with localized products, regulatory compliance, and stablecoin support for each region.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Canada">
    **Full market launch targeting 50,000\+ users and \$100M\+ CAD in assets under management.**

    Buburuza obtains full licensing from the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI), including provincial licenses for Quebec and other provinces. The platform launches with complete French-Canadian language support and culturally adapted services.

    - Full OSFI banking license and provincial compliance
    - French-Canadian localization across the entire app
    - Partnerships with Canadian financial institutions and payment processors
    - Timeline: Regulatory approvals in Q3, soft launch in Q4 with full market opening
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="European Union">
    **EU-wide rollout targeting 200,000\+ users and €500M\+ in assets under management.**

    Buburuza achieves full MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets) compliance, obtains Electronic Money Institution (EMI) and Payment Institution (PI) licenses, and registers as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider (CASP). EURB — the EUR-backed stablecoin — launches with 1:1 EURC reserves and SEPA integration.

    - MiCA-compliant operations across all 27 member states
    - Localization for German, French, Spanish, Italian, and Dutch markets
    - SEPA integration through EU banking partnerships
    - Enhanced GDPR compliance for EU data protection
    - Timeline: Germany, France, and the Netherlands in Q3; full EU-27 rollout in Q4
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="MENA">
    **Regional launch in UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt targeting 75,000\+ users.**

    Buburuza launches UAE operations under a Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) license, participates in the Saudi Central Bank regulatory sandbox, and enters Egypt through local banking partnerships. AEDB — the AED-backed stablecoin — goes live with Shariah-compliant product options.

    - DIFC license for UAE operations
    - Saudi Central Bank regulatory sandbox participation
    - Arabic language support across the full platform
    - Shariah-compliant financial products
    - AEDB stablecoin optimized for AED transactions
    - Timeline: UAE pilot in Q3; Saudi Arabia and Egypt launch in Q4
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Natural language financial interface

You'll be able to manage your finances entirely through conversation — text or voice — in your own language. The natural language interface understands multi-step financial instructions, generates reports on demand, and can walk you through complex planning scenarios in plain terms.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask in natural language">
    Type or say what you need — "Show me how my portfolio would look if I moved 20% into EURB" — and the system interprets it with full financial context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive a personalized response">
    The AI draws on your account history, current market data, and your stated goals to give you a specific, actionable answer rather than a generic one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute directly">
    Confirm the action through biometric authentication and the transaction executes instantly — no navigating menus or filling out forms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and learn">
    The interface retains context across sessions, building a clearer picture of your financial priorities over time to give better recommendations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Supported interactions include:** complex portfolio planning, financial concept explanations, automated account onboarding, real-time financial coaching, and report generation.

### Cross-platform integration AI

This system connects Buburuza to traditional banks, DeFi protocols, and enterprise platforms through a centralized API gateway. An intelligent routing layer finds the optimal path for every transaction across all connected networks, while a yield optimization engine moves funds automatically to the highest-returning opportunities within your risk parameters.

- **Traditional banking:** Account aggregation and direct transfers with major banks
- **DeFi protocols:** Automated lending, borrowing, and yield farming interactions
- **Enterprise systems:** ERP, accounting, and treasury management integrations
- **Unified reporting:** Consolidated view of all connected accounts and positions in one place
