### Compliance AI agent

Traditional compliance is slow, opaque, and expensive. Buburuza's Compliance AI Agent changes that by running continuous, real-time analysis on all platform transactions — processing over 40,000 per second — using machine learning models that adapt to evolving regulatory requirements across jurisdictions.

Zero-knowledge proofs let the agent verify compliance without accessing more of your personal data than is strictly necessary. The agent surfaces patterns that require review and prepares the underlying case file, so compliance analysts reach a decision faster.

**What this means for you:** Your account stays compliant in every market Buburuza operates in, without you needing to understand local regulations or submit paperwork manually.

### Transaction AI agent

Every payment you make is analyzed in real time to find the optimal route — balancing speed, cost, and current network conditions. The agent's behavioral fraud models run in under 100 milliseconds, catching fraudulent patterns before a transaction completes rather than flagging it after the fact.

For cross-border payments, the agent handles currency conversion automatically, selects the path with the narrowest spread, and ensures the transaction meets regulatory requirements in both the origin and destination country.

**What this means for you:** You send money and it arrives quickly, at a lower cost, with fraud stopped before it reaches your account.

<Tip>
  Cross-border payments support 50\+ fiat currencies and all major cryptocurrencies. The agent continuously monitors exchange rates and liquidity across multiple venues to get you the best available rate at the moment you transact.
</Tip>

### Security AI agent

Behavioral biometrics build a unique profile of how you interact with the app — how you swipe, how quickly you type, the patterns in your session timing. Any deviation from that profile triggers an anomaly alert. The agent cross-references threat intelligence feeds and applies risk-based authentication dynamically, adding friction only when it's warranted.

Automated incident response means threats are contained within seconds, not minutes. The agent escalates to you with a clear notification and the ability to take immediate action from the app.

**What this means for you:** Your account is protected by something attackers can't steal — your behavioral fingerprint. Current model accuracy is published in the [model version table](/api/ai-services#model-versions).

### Customer support AI agent

The support agent understands financial queries in multiple languages with full context awareness — it knows your account history, recent transactions, and any open issues before you type a single word. Common questions are resolved instantly. Complex or sensitive matters are routed to the right human specialist with full context already transferred, so you never have to repeat yourself.

**What this means for you:** Support that answers in seconds, 24/7, in your language, and knows who you are.

## Advanced banking features

The AI agent layer enables a set of banking features that would be impossible to deliver safely at scale without automated intelligence underneath them.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Autonomous portfolio management">
    Buburuza analyzes your financial situation and goals to build a personalized risk profile, then manages your portfolio automatically — rebalancing allocations, optimizing for tax efficiency, and integrating ESG factors if you choose them. You set the parameters; the platform handles the execution.

    - AI-driven rebalancing to maintain your target allocations
    - Tax-loss harvesting timed intelligently throughout the year
    - Optional ESG screening for sustainable investing
    - Continuous performance monitoring with clear reporting
    - Real-time integration with major exchanges and DeFi protocols
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Predictive cash flow analysis">
    Machine learning models trained on your transaction history forecast your income and expenses weeks or months ahead. You can model the impact of financial decisions — changing jobs, making a large purchase, starting a business — before you commit to them. Forecasting horizons range from one month to 30 years.

    - Income and expense prediction based on your patterns
    - Interactive scenario planning tools
    - Budget optimization recommendations
    - Business cash flow forecasting with seasonal adjustments
    - Long-term financial goal planning with milestone tracking
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Smart contract financial products">
    Loans, insurance, and investment agreements execute automatically through smart contracts deployed on Buburuza-Chain. Terms are encoded directly into the contract, so you don't need a counterparty to manually approve every step. Compliance is built into each contract type.

    - Automated personal and business lending with dynamic rates
    - Parametric insurance with instant claim processing
    - Smart escrow for complex transactions
    - Investment agreements with automated execution
    - Dispute resolution mechanisms built in
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Optimized cross-border payments">
    The Transaction AI Agent finds the optimal path for every international transfer — across correspondent banking networks and crypto rails — in real time. You see the exact fee and estimated arrival time before you confirm. Settlement happens in the background.

    - Intelligent routing across 50\+ fiat currencies
    - Real-time exchange rates with minimal spread
    - Full fee disclosure before you send
    - Automated regulatory documentation for international transfers
    - Real-time tracking from send to receipt
  </Accordion>
