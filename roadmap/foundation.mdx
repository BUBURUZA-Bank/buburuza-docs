### Buburuza-Chain mainnet optimization

The network's consensus mechanism was tuned specifically for high-throughput financial operations. Advanced transaction batching, parallel processing, and gas optimization algorithms work together so you never wait for a confirmation or pay more in fees than necessary.

<Note>
  The mainnet is designed to maintain 99.99% uptime. Comprehensive monitoring and alerting systems track performance continuously, and stress testing under peak-load conditions validated that the 10,000\+ TPS target is sustainable.
</Note>

### Enhanced security framework

Security operates at multiple layers simultaneously. Behavioral anomaly detection flags unusual activity before it becomes a problem, audit logs capture every operation for compliance purposes, and quantum-resistant cryptographic foundations protect the platform against emerging threats.

<Tip>
  You can review your account's activity log at any time from the app. Any flagged event triggers an immediate notification to you — you're always the first to know.
</Tip>

### Cross-chain bridge optimization

Buburuza's EVM compatibility layer lets you move assets across chains without using a third-party bridge. Redundant validators keep the bridge available at 99.9%, and cross-chain transaction batching reduces the cost of each transfer.

### Fireblocks custody integration

Multi-party computation means no single key — or single point of failure — controls your assets. Automated key management, rotation schedules, and disaster recovery procedures are all built into the custody layer, giving you the same protections used by institutional investors.

### Regulatory compliance framework

Buburuza uses Sumsub to power its KYC and AML workflows. Zero-knowledge proof technology lets Buburuza verify your identity and compliance status without storing more personal data than necessary. Suspicious activity monitoring runs continuously, and reporting to regulatory bodies across multiple jurisdictions is fully automated.

### Multi-stablecoin infrastructure

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="USDB">
    US Dollar-backed stablecoin with 1:1 USDC reserves. Use USDB for global payments, savings, and cross-border transfers in USD.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="EURB">
    Euro-backed stablecoin with 1:1 EURC reserves. Purpose-built for EU transactions and European market expansion.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="AEDB">

  </Tab>

The design provides for on-chain reserve verification and independent attestation of backing. Neither is in place yet.

### Fiat on/off ramp

Multiple payment providers are integrated with automated intelligent routing — Buburuza selects the path that gives you the best rate and fastest settlement. Compliance checks for large transactions run automatically in the background so you experience no unnecessary friction.
