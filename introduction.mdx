Buburuza is a new kind of financial platform. Instead of layering technology on top of legacy infrastructure, Buburuza builds artificial intelligence, blockchain settlement, and regulatory compliance together from the start—so every feature works the way you'd actually expect it to. You get instant global transfers, intelligent fraud protection, tokenized real-world investments, and an account that never sleeps.

<Note>
  Buburuza is a financial technology company, not a bank. Regulated money movement is performed by licensed partners. See [Legal and regulatory](/legal) for who holds which permission.
</Note>

## What Buburuza solves for you

Traditional banks were built for a world where humans reviewed every transaction and cross-border wires took days. Fintech apps improved the experience but kept the same underlying constraints. Buburuza is different in three concrete ways.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The missing middle between banking and DeFi" icon="scale-balanced">
    You shouldn't have to choose between the predictability of established payment rails and the speed of a crypto protocol. Buburuza gives you both: IBANs and SWIFT/SEPA wires alongside instant stablecoin transfers, programmable money, and on-chain settlement—all in one account.

    <Warning>
      Buburuza is not a bank and does not offer deposit insurance. Funds are held with regulated payment and custody partners. Balances are not insured by the FDIC, CDIC, or any other deposit insurance scheme.

  <Accordion title="Stable, accessible real-world asset investments" icon="chart-mixed">

Through Buburuza-Labs, you can invest in operational industrial assets—starting from $$1 per token—with monthly revenue distributions paid directly to your wallet.

  <Accordion title="A financial layer for the autonomous economy" icon="robot">

## How the AI works for you

Buburuza operates on a "suggest-confirm" model. The AI handles routine operations automatically—routing your payment through the fastest available path, detecting an unusual login attempt, or flagging a duplicate bill. For higher-stakes decisions—large transfers, account changes, investment orders—you confirm before anything executes.

<Note>
  Your personal data stays completely isolated from other users. Every AI agent operates in a dedicated processing environment, and your account context is never shared across accounts.
</Note>

This means you get 24/7 banking that responds instantly to routine events, with meaningful human control exactly where it matters. Low-risk operations run automatically. Medium-risk operations prompt a quick confirmation. High-risk situations escalate to human oversight or pause until you intervene.
