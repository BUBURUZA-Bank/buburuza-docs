# What is Buburuza?

Buburuza is a **payments platform for organisations**. A company opens one account, passes KYB verification, and gets:

- a **fiat account** with ACH and wire funding rails,
- **stablecoin wallets** for crypto top-ups and transfers,
- a **REST API** (`/api/v1`) for automating money movement,
- built-in **compliance**: KYB/KYC verification, transaction monitoring, and travel-rule reporting.

<Note>
  Buburuza is a financial technology company, not a bank. Regulated money movement is performed by licensed partners. See [Legal and regulatory](/legal) for who holds which permission.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create an account with an email address. Access is passwordless: a passkey (WebAuthn) is the primary credential, with TOTP as a fallback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYB">
    Verify your organisation through our KYB flow. Verification is performed by a licensed identity provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your payment account">
    After KYB passes, your organisation's payment account is provisioned automatically and your fiat and stablecoin wallets are created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move money">
    Top up by ACH, wire, or crypto. Send transfers to other wallets and payouts to external recipients, with fee previews before every send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who it's for

- **Businesses** that need a regulated account to hold fiat and stablecoins and pay suppliers, contractors, or partners.
- **Developers** who want a payments API: accounts, wallets, transfers, recipients, and transaction history under one contract.
- **Platforms** that embed money movement into their own products.

The canonical API contract (OpenAPI 3.x, 78 endpoints) is maintained alongside the platform and is the authoritative reference for request and response shapes.

## Platform capabilities

| Capability | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Onboarding & KYB** | Step-based organisation onboarding, document checklist, provider-hosted verification, payment-account provisioning |
| **Accounts & wallets** | Fiat account (ACH / wire) and stablecoin wallets per organisation, decimal-string balances (`available`, `pending`, `hold`) |
| **Funding** | Deposit instructions per rail; fee previews; inbound funds appear in the transaction list automatically |
| **Transfers & payouts** | Wallet-to-wallet transfers with bound fee quotes (fee on top); withdrawals to external recipients |
| **Recipients & beneficiaries** | Reusable payee records, linking to provider recipients |
| **Transactions** | Full history with filters by account, wallet, kind, and status |
| **Security** | Passkeys, optional MFA, session management, signed webhooks between services |
| **Compliance** | KYB tiers, transaction monitoring, travel-rule reporting |
